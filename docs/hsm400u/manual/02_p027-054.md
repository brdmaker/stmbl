# Mikron HSM 400U manual, pages 27-54: handling and operation

Source: `Mikron_ohjeet_s27_54.pdf` (28 PDF pages = manual pages 27-54). Scanned, clearly legible.

Original language: English.

These pages cover chapter 4 from 4.1 to 4.5.9:
- the operating panel and the machine key designations (S/Q/A numbers)
- start-up and switch-off, EMERGENCY STOP, power ON/OFF and door lock
- spindle pre-lubrication and warm-up
- reference point approach and the NC operating modes
- jog, handwheel and rapid traverse
- spindle, coolant, spray gun, cooling unit and chip conveyor
- the SET-UP / SET-UP extended key-switch modes and the F1/F2 keys
- spindle programming and orientation
- NC stop and program resumption

Most of this is PLC behaviour that a LinuxCNC retrofit would have to reproduce.

## 4. Handling and operation

### 4.1 Operating elements / 4.1.1 Operating panel (p. 27-29)

  - C screen layout

  - G feed potentiometer
  - H machining/programming mode switch
  - I handwheel
  - X key switch for extended set-up mode
- Spindle potentiometer F: **70-120 %** of the programmed speed in all modes. The SPU maximum speed cannot be exceeded. In SET-UP extended mode the maximum is **800 rpm**.

### 4.2 Machine start up / switch off (p. 30-34)

#### 4.2.1 EMERGENCY STOP

- E-STOP key **S121** works in every mode. The machine stops as fast as possible, shows "00 EMERGENCY STOP BUTTON", and releases the protection door once stopped.
- The control can trigger E-STOP internally. **Error messages 00-29 are E-STOP messages** (see "PLC messages").
- Cancel:
  1. Eliminate all faults.
  2. Unlock the E-STOP key.
  3. Clear the messages with CE.
  4. When "62 WAIT PLEASE" disappears, open and close the protection door.
  5. Press POWER ON/OFF (S/H171).
- Note: at high speeds a restart may be needed after E-STOP: main switch Q111 OFF, wait about **15 s**, then ON.

#### 4.2.2 Switching ON

1. Turn on the main switch Q111. The control runs a memory test and then shows "POWER INTERRUPTED".
2. Make sure all E-STOP keys are unlocked, then press CE. The PLC program compiles and the control enters "TRAVERSE REFERENCE POINTS".
3. Clear "EXTERNAL EMERGENCY STOP" with CE.
4. Approach the reference points (4.2.7).

- Note: the oil/air spindle (**30,000 or 42,000 rpm**) needs pre-lubrication.
  - "79 LUBRICATION SPINDLE AKTIVE" is shown at every start-up.
  - Meanwhile axes may be referenced and jogged in manual mode.
  - Spindle start and full program run are blocked until pre-lubrication ends.

#### 4.2.3 Switching OFF

1. Abort the program and select MANUAL OPERATION.
2. Move the axes to suitable positions.
3. Second soft-key row: "OFF", then "YES". Wait for "NOW YOU CAN SWITCH OFF THE TNC".
4. Turn Q111 OFF and padlock it if necessary.

- Warning: never operate Q111 while the spindle turns or axes move (equipment failure or data loss).

#### 4.2.4 Power ON/OFF (S/H171)

- Locks and unlocks the protection door. Valid in all modes.
- Power OFF sequence: feed axes stop, spindle stops, spray cooling and chip conveyor (CRU) stop, then the door unlocks. Lamp H171 lit = access permitted.
- S/H171 is inactive during:
  - tapping with floating holder
  - tapping
  - spindle positioning (until in position)
  - automatic tool change
  - TC troubleshooting
  - automatic workpiece change
  - WPC recovery mode
- Power ON:
  1. Open and close the door (initializes the safety circuit).
  2. Press S/H171. When lamp H171 goes OFF, power is on.
- Note: wait about **2 s** between opening and closing the door.

#### 4.2.4.1 Door lock initializing

1. Open and close one protection door.
2. Press S/H171. When H171 goes OFF, the drives are ON.

#### 4.2.5 Lubrication of the spindles

- Pre-lubrication time:
  - **10 min** if the machine was off for more than 15 min, or if a lubrication aggregate fault lasted more than 15 min.
  - **1 lubricating pulse** if a fault lasted less than 15 min with the control active, or if the machine was off for less than 15 min.
- During pre-lubrication:
  - The next cycle starts 1 min after the previous one completes.
  - The message is "79 PRELUBRICATION SPINDLE ACTIVE" and spindle start is barred.
- After pre-lubrication, the next cycle starts every **2 min** after completion.
- Lubricating pulse:
  - When lubrication pressure is reached, the pump runs on for **4 s**.
  - If pressure is not reached within **25 s**: internal NC-STOP and "38 ERROR SPINDLE LUBRICATION".
  - The same error appears if **air pressure** is too low for more than **20 s**.
  - If pressure does not drop within **25 s** after the pump stops: internal NC-STOP and "38 ERROR SPINDLE LUBRICATION".
  - Level monitor triggered: "78 CHECK SPINDLE LUBRICATION".

#### 4.2.6 Spindle unit (SPU) warm-up

- Work speed ≤ **21,000 rpm**: warm up **6 min at 21,000 rpm**.
- Work speed above 21,000 rpm: **3 min at 21,000 rpm**, then **3 min at the work speed**.
- If the water inlet temperature is below **20 °C**, warm it with motor losses:
  1. Check that all monitoring is OK.
  2. Run the spindle at **3,000 rpm** until the water reaches 20 °C.
- See the spindle maker's manual.

#### 4.2.7 Approach of reference points

- 4.2.7.1 Manual approach (when the Mikron sequence risks a collision):
  - Press the axis direction key until the reference mark is passed: about **20 mm** for X/Y/Z, up to **20°** for rotary axes.
  - The axis is then removed from the "not referenced" display.
  - Note: if X/Y/Z is less than 20 mm from the end position, or the swivel axis is near its end, the direction toward that end is barred for referencing.
- 4.2.7.2 Automatic sequence: **Z, Y, X, 4th, 5th**. Each axis is started with NC START S520. Afterwards the control switches to MANUAL OPERATION.
- 4.2.7.3 Allowed in TRAVERSE REFERENCE POINTS mode:
  - spindle start with active speed
  - axis traverse
  - opening and closing the doors
  - tool retention operation with the door open
  - spray cooling ON (S411)
  - **chucks 1 + 2 ON/OFF**
- 4.2.7.4 Before full referencing, other modes may be used for:
  - spindle start
  - M functions
  - moving the referenced axes
  - changing spindle speed in manual mode
  - doors
  - tool retention with the door open
- Soft key "TRAVERSE REFERENCE POINTS" stays available in MANUAL and EL. HANDWHEEL until all axes are referenced.

### 4.3 NC operating modes (p. 35)

- Programming modes: PROGRAMMING AND EDITING; TEST RUN (simulation graphics).
- Machine modes, manual: MANUAL OPERATION (axis keys, datum, incremental jog); EL. HANDWHEEL (handwheel and axis keys, incremental jog).
- Machine modes, automatic: POSITIONING WITH MANUAL INPUT (MDI); PROGRAM RUN FULL SEQUENCE; PROGRAM RUN SINGLE BLOCK.

#### 4.3.1 Automatic operating mode "axes limit switches" (p. 36)

- "Automatic" = all functions available with the door closed and locked.
- Software limit switches (machine parameters, active after referencing) lock the machine if hit in a program.
- To recover:
  1. Clear with CE.
  2. Move away in the opposite direction with the axis key.

#### 4.3.1.1 Traversing the axes (p. 36-41)

- MANUAL OPERATION: one axis at a time with the direction key. At a software limit a message appears and the axis stops; the message clears when moving back.
- Incremental jog (EL. HANDWHEEL):
  - Soft key "INCREMENT ON/OFF"; the PLC window shows "INCREMENT JOG POSITIONING: ON".
  - Each key press moves one increment.
  - Example: 8 mm increment → 8 mm per press, 16 mm after two presses.
- Feed limit: soft key "F" plus ENT. Override 0-150 %.
- Warning: all axis direction keys are live; pressing one moves the axis immediately.
- Handwheel axis select: orange keys X, Y, Z, IV, V. A handwheel symbol marks the active axis.
- Crank direction (as printed): **clockwise = negative direction, counter-clockwise = positive direction**.

Handwheel division factor (travel per crank revolution):

| Division factor | mm per revolution |
|---|---|
| 3 | 2.5 |
| 4 | 1.25 |
| 5 | 0.625 |
| 6 | 0.313 |
| 7 | 0.156 |
| 8 | 0.078 |
| 9 | 0.039 |
| 10 | 0.020 |

Set it by entering the factor at "DIVISION FACTOR:" and pressing ENT.

**External handwheel HR 336E (Heidenhain, option, p. 39-40):**

| No. | Element | Function |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mushroom push button, locking | EMERGENCY STOP |
| 2 / 2.1 | Push buttons right / left | Approval keys for axis motion with the door open |
| 3 | Shaft encoder | Electronic handwheel |
| 4 | Axis direction keys with LED | Axis select X, Y, Z, IV, V; jog +/- at the feed potentiometer rate |
| 5 | Release key | Must be held with the axis key to jog in EL. HANDWHEEL mode |

- With the HR 336E connected, the panel handwheel A524 is disabled.
- Active-axis LEDs light up; they blink in incremental jog.
- Jogging with the direction keys in EL. HANDWHEEL needs approval keys 2 and 2.1 pressed together.
- Note: in the SET-UP modes the approval keys are needed for all functions.

**Rapid traverse key S303 (p. 41):**
- Moves the feed axes at maximum speed. The feed potentiometer still scales the rapid speed.
- Inactive during tapping, reference approach, with the 3D probe ON, and in SET-UP mode.
- Warning: no machining in rapid traverse.

#### 4.3.1.2 Spindle unit (SPU) (p. 42-43)

- Spindle start key S211 (manual modes): starts clockwise at the programmed speed. With no valid speed it starts at **20 min⁻¹**.
- Spindle stop key S212:
  - Manual modes: decelerates in about **3 s**.
  - Automatic modes: NC stop, then feeds stop, then spindle and coolant stop.
  - Inactive during the same functions as S/H171: tapping (floating/rigid), spindle positioning, automatic TC, TC troubleshooting, automatic WPC change, WPC recovery.
- Internal NC stops: **error messages 30-49 are NC STOP messages**.
- Speed input in EL. HANDWHEEL or MANUAL mode:
  1. Soft key "S".
  2. Enter **100-60,000 rpm**.
  3. Press NC START S520.
- A gear change may happen depending on the gear stage.
- Start with S211 or M03/M04/M13/M14. Stop with S212 or M00/M02/M05/M06/M30.
- A new speed while running changes speed without stopping.

#### 4.3.1.3 Spray gun ON/OFF (p. 43)

- Key **S417** switches the spray gun pump on; the trigger on the gun releases the jet.
- The pump runs exactly **1 min** and then switches off. Pressing S417 again switches it off early.

#### 4.3.1.4 NC-Start (p. 44)

- Key **S520** starts: reference approach, NC program, probing cycles, MDI positioning blocks, spindle speed selection, M functions.
- Heidenhain calls it the "machine START button".

#### 4.3.1.5 NC-Stop (p. 45)

- Key **S521**:
  - MANUAL OPERATION: stops continuous axis traverse.
  - Automatic modes: NC stop.
- Inactive during floating tapping, spindle positioning, automatic TC, TC troubleshooting, automatic WPC change, WPC recovery. The manual says "SPINDLE STOP key (S521)" here [sic].
- Heidenhain calls it the "machine STOP button".

#### 4.3.1.6 Cooling unit (CU) for spindle, electric cabinet (ELC) and feed motors (p. 45)

- Pump motor **M426** of the active circuit turns on with the main switch plus CE and runs continuously while working.
- It turns off on an E-STOP key press, and in stand-by **5 min after M77**.

#### 4.3.1.7 Coolant emulsion (p. 46-47)

- Key **S411**:
  - Manual modes: toggles emulsion on and off. It also switches off coolants started by M07, M08, M13, M14 or M28.
  - Automatic modes: with no coolant active, S411 toggles **coolant no. 1**; switching off shows "69 COOLANT SWITCHED OFF" (clear with CE). Coolants started by M07/M08/M13/M14/M28 can be switched off with S411, and pressing it again turns them back on unless the program changed them.
- **Coolant blow-out (spray cooling)**: defined by **Cycle 300** and switched with **M31 ON / M32 OFF**. Used during tool change and Blum laser measurement to stop coolant dripping on the TC or laser.
- Cycle 300 is in MDI or PROGRAMMING with CYCL DEF on the second soft-key row. Parameters:
  - **Q397** = dwell between coolant OFF and blow-out start [s]
  - **Q398** = blow-out duration [s]
  - **Q399** = number of blow-outs
- Examples:
  - Conversational: `CYCLE DEF 300 COOLANT BLOW-OUT`, Q397=2, Q398=1, Q399=1.
  - ISO: `N10 G300 Q397=2 98=1 Q399=1` (as printed).
- The cycle is modal and reset by M2/M30.
- Defaults at start-up: Q397=0.0, Q398=1.0, Q399=1. Q399=0 = no blow-out.
- The **TC work-area door opens only after the blow-out cycle has completed.**

#### 4.3.1.8 Chip removal unit (CRU) (p. 48)

- Always two chip conveyors at right angles.
- Selector switch: 0 = both OFF; 1 = automatic ON (latching); 2 = reverse (momentary, active only while held).
- When jammed in automatic: motor protection switch **Q471** trips and the screen shows "73 CHIP CONVEYOR INACTIVE" (see 4.8.4, error 73).
- The conveyors stop automatically in SET-UP with drives off and door unlocked, and on E-STOP.
- Warning: unplug the CRU before removing it for repair or cleaning.
- Warning: never clear jammed chips by hand; use gloves and a chip hook.
- See 5.3.6 and 8.1.

#### 4.3.1.9 Protection cabin (CAB) (p. 48)

- The door opens and closes manually.
- Automatic operation needs the door **closed and locked**. The door is monitored by safety switches.

#### 4.3.2 Key switch / approval key (S124): special mode SET-UP (p. 49)

1. Press S/H171. When lamp H171 is ON, the drives stop and the doors unlock.
2. Open a door.
3. Wait about **2 s** (safety circuit initialization), then press approval key **S124**; the drives come back on. "66 SET-UP" is shown.

- Available in SET-UP:
  - axis traverse
  - external cooling via S411
  - probing with the 3D probe
  - M functions except **M03, M04, M13, M14, M19**
- Approval keys: S124 on the panel, or the two side keys on the portable handwheel.
- MANUAL and EL. HANDWHEEL can be switched in SET-UP.
- Warning: to stop, first release the axis key or crank, then the approval key. Otherwise the axis monitoring may switch the control off and the machine must be restarted.
- Note: SET-UP stays active for **3 min only**. Re-arm by releasing the approval keys and waiting about **2 s**. Main-axis feed is limited to **2000 mm/min**.
- End SET-UP:
  1. Release the approval keys.
  2. When H171 goes OFF, close the doors.
  3. Press S/H171.

#### 4.3.3 Key switch S172: SET-UP and SET-UP extended (p. 50-51)

- Positions:
  - Normal = program run and axis motion with the door closed.
  - **I** = SET-UP.
  - **II** = SET-UP EXTENDED.
- The key can be removed in Normal and I.
- SET-UP extended = SET-UP plus spindle start for aligning and scratching, **max 800 rpm**.
- Activate:
  1. Turn S172 and press S/H171. Lamp H171 ON: drives stop and doors unlock.
  2. Open a door, wait about **2 s**, then press the approval key. The spindle speed is set to **200 rpm**; "67 SET-UP MODE EXTENDED" is shown.
- Allowed functions:
  - axis traverse
  - spindle up to 800 rpm
  - spray cooling S411
  - 3D probe
  - M functions except **M07, M21, M28, M60, M61**
- With the approval keys held, speeds **80-800 rpm** can be set with soft key "S".
- The spindle stops on approval key release, S212, or M05.
- Warning: do not operate S172 while the spindle turns, or the spindle will not decelerate in a controlled manner.
- End:
  1. Release the approval keys.
  2. When H171 goes OFF, close the doors.
  3. Press S/H171.
  4. Turn S172 back and pull out the key.

#### 4.3.4 Function keys F1 and F2 (p. 51)

- **F1 (S401)**: clamp/unclamp the WPC pallet chuck on the table. Active only with the cabin door open.
- **F2 (S402)**: optional, assignable.

### 4.4 Programming (p. 52)

- 4.4.1 TOOL CALL dialogue (PROGRAMMING AND EDITING / MDI):
  - tool number **0-65535**
  - spindle axis X/Y/Z (orange key)
  - spindle speed S **50-42,000 rpm**
- Start with M03, M04, M13 or M14.
- Stop with M00, M02, M05, M06, M30, TOOL CALL or a workpiece change.
- Warning: respect tool ratings and balance to G 2.5 (VDI 2060 / ISO 1940).
- 4.4.2 Spindle orientation:
  - **0-360°**, input resolution **0.1°**.
  - Angle set by Cycle 13, executed by **M19**. Example: `CYCL DEF 13.0 ORIENTATION` / `13.1 ANGLE 45` / `M19`.
  - Without a defined angle, M19 orients to 0°.

### 4.5 Program run (p. 53-54)

- 4.5.1 Interrupting an automatic program:
  - **S521**: feed axes stop.
  - **S212**: NC stop, feeds stop, then spindle and coolants stop.
  - **S/H171**: NC stop, feeds stop, then spindle and coolants stop, then the door is released (H171 = access allowed).
- No NC stop is possible during floating or rigid tapping, spindle positioning, automatic TC, TC troubleshooting, automatic WPC change or WPC troubleshooting.
- Error messages 30-49 = internal NC STOP messages.
- 4.5.2 Program interruption:
  - Mid-block NC stop (blinking asterisk): soft key "INTERNAL STOP".
  - In MDI or SINGLE BLOCK at the end of a block: CE.
- 4.5.3 Resume:
  1. If needed, open and close the door, then press S/H171.
  2. Clear the message with CE.
  3. Press NC START S520.
- 4.5.4 Rigid tapping interrupted (Heidenhain cycles **17 or 207**): soft key MANUAL OPERATION, then retract the tap under NC control with **Z+**.
- 4.5.5 Moving axes during NC stop (other cases):
  1. NC stop with S521 or S212.
  2. Soft key MANUAL OPERATION.
  3. Move as in EL. HANDWHEEL.
  4. Doors can then be unlocked with S/H171.
- 4.5.6 Reapproach the contour: returns axes moved during the NC stop.
- 4.5.7 Any sequence:
  1. Close the doors and press S/H171 if needed.
  2. Soft key "RESTORE POSITION".
  3. "RESTORE X", "RESTORE Y", and so on, each with S520.
  4. S520 to continue machining.
- 4.5.8 With position logic:
  1. Close the doors and press S/H171.
  2. "RESTORE POSITION".
  3. S520 restores the axes in the set order.
  4. S520 again to continue.
- 4.5.9 Block scan:
  - Starts at the current block, so tool, feed and other status must be valid; starting from the program beginning is recommended.
  - Nested PGM-CALL programs must be scanned from the main program.
  - Not executed during a block scan: Tool Def, M00, M02, M30, M77.

## For the retrofit

- **Panel I/O to rewire into the LinuxCNC HAL/PLC:**
  - S121 E-STOP
  - S124 approval (enabling) key
  - S/H171 power/door key with lamp H171
  - S172 3-position key switch
  - S520/S521 NC start/stop
  - S211/S212 spindle on/off
  - S303 rapid
  - S401/S402 F1/F2
  - S411 coolant
  - S417 spray gun
  - axis keys ±X..±V
  - feed override 0-150 % and spindle override 70-120 %
  - handwheel A524; optional HR 336E with E-STOP, two approval keys, release key and LED axis keys
  - main switch Q111
- **E-STOP and safety sequences to reproduce:**
  - E-STOP stops as fast as possible, then releases the door. Reset = CE, then cycle the door, then S/H171.
  - Door safety circuit: open/close with about 2 s minimum between, then S/H171; drives on when H171 goes off.
  - Door locked during automatic operation.
  - SET-UP mode: drives off, door open, approval key brings the drives back; max **2000 mm/min**; 3 min time-out; M03/M04/M13/M14/M19 blocked.
  - SET-UP extended: spindle **80-800 rpm**, default 200 rpm, with the approval key held; M07/M21/M28/M60/M61 blocked.
  - S/H171, S212 and S521 are ignored during tapping, spindle orientation, TC and WPC sequences.
  - Main switch restart after E-STOP at high speed: off for about 15 s.
- **Spindle (original drive kept):**
  - Versions 30,000 or 42,000 rpm. Program range 50-42,000 rpm (MDI/TOOL CALL); manual "S" input range 100-60,000 rpm.
  - Default 20 min⁻¹ if no speed is set. Manual stop decel about 3 s.
  - Orientation M19 with 0.1° resolution, angle from Cycle 13.
  - A "gear stage" is mentioned. Probably not applicable to a motor spindle, but the PLC must handle it if present [uncertain].
  - The new control must give the spindle drive speed, direction and enable, plus orient and at-speed signals, and keep the interlocks: pre-lubrication done, tool clamped, door state.
- **Spindle oil/air lubrication logic (must be reimplemented):**
  - Pre-lube: 10 min if off for more than 15 min, otherwise 1 pulse. Cycle every 1 min during pre-lube, every 2 min afterwards.
  - 4 s pump run-on after pressure is reached.
  - Error if pressure is not reached in 25 s, or not relieved 25 s after the pump stops, or air pressure is low for more than 20 s. A level switch gives a warning.
  - Spindle start inhibited during pre-lube.
- **Warm-up:** 6 min at 21,000 rpm (or 3 + 3 min). If the coolant water is below 20 °C, run at 3,000 rpm until 20 °C. This implies a water temperature sensor the control reads.
- **Homing:**
  - Order **Z, Y, X, 4th, 5th**, each started separately.
  - Linear reference marks within about 20 mm, rotary within 20°. Distance-coded scales, see pages 1-26.
  - No reference move toward an end stop when less than 20 mm from it.
  - Software limits are in machine parameters.
- **Axis names:** X, Y, Z, IV, V (4th/5th = B/C on the HSM 400U).
- **Handwheel:** clockwise = negative per the manual. Division factors 3-10 give 2.5-0.020 mm/rev.
- **Auxiliaries the PLC switches:**
  - Cooling unit pump **M426** (on with main switch plus CE; off on E-STOP or 5 min after M77 stand-by). It also cools the ELC and the feed motors.
  - Coolant emulsion (coolant no. 1; M07/M08/M13/M14/M28).
  - Spray gun pump S417 (1 min timer).
  - Air blow-out M31/M32 with Cycle 300 parameters Q397/Q398/Q399.
  - Two chip conveyors with forward/reverse and motor protection Q471.
  - Pallet chuck clamp F1/S401 (door-open only) and "chucks 1 + 2".
  - TC work-area door, which opens only after blow-out completes.
- **M-codes and messages referenced:** M00, M02, M03, M04, M05, M06, M07, M08, M13, M14, M19, M21, M28, M30, M31, M32, M60, M61, M77.
- **PLC message numbering:** 00-29 E-STOP class, 30-49 NC-STOP class. Messages seen here: 00, 20, 38, 62, 66, 67, 69, 73, 78, 79.
