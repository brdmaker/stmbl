# Mikron HSM 400U manual, pages 1-26: preface, safety, design and function

Source: `Mikron_ohjeet_s1_26.pdf` (34 PDF pages: cover, blank page, foreword, 5 pages of contents, manual pages 1-26). Scanned, clearly legible.

Original language: English (Mikron's English operating manual). Only two handwritten notes on the cover are in Finnish.

These pages cover the title page, the full table of contents of the 174-page manual, the preface, abbreviations, general safety instructions (ch. 2) and the machine description (ch. 3). Chapter 3 covers the axis drives, spindle, measuring systems, tool changer, workpiece changer, cabin, operating panel, electric cabinet, lubrication and hydraulics, pneumatics, and the safety devices (E-STOP, door lock, software limits, spindle clamp sensor). There are few numbers. The useful hardware facts are the encoder and scale arrangement, the hydraulic pressures and the 10 m/s² acceleration.

## 1. Preface (p. 1-3)

- The manual must be read by everyone working on the machine. Copying it or passing it to third parties needs Mikron's consent.
- 1.1 Target groups:
  - Mechanical work: trained mechanic.
  - Electrical work: trained electrician or electronics engineer.
  - Operators: mechanics training recommended. They must observe safety rules and confirm by signature that they have read the safety chapter.
  - Service engineers: trained to Mikron standards.
  - Everyone needs basic NC/CNC knowledge.
- 1.2 Customer training: maintenance training, programming courses and application courses (contact Mikron Nidau).
- 1.3 Conventions:
  - Bold text = operating modes.
  - "Quoted capitals" = messages and soft keys.
  - Three symbols: Danger (triangle), Warning (raised finger: correct sequence to avoid damage), Note (pointing hand: special technical feature).
- 1.4 Abbreviations:
  - Main assemblies: AXU axis unit, BM basic machine, HYP hydraulic/pneumatic, MB machine base, PC process components, PD protection devices, SPU spindle unit, TOH tool handling, WPH workpiece handling.
  - Subassemblies: CAB cabin, CB chip box, CORU coolant reconditioning unit, COU control unit, CRU chip removal unit, CU cooling unit, DEU dust extraction unit, DRC drive components, ELC electric cabinet, ICS internal cooling system, MLU minimum lubrication unit, MPL mounting plate, OMEU oil mist extraction unit, OP operating panel, PT pallet table, RT rotary table, RTT rotary and tilting table, TC tool changer, TPR touch probe, TT tilting table, WPC workpiece changer.

## 2. Safety instructions (p. 4-9)

- 2.1 Intended use:
  - Milling and drilling of materials that allow chip removal only. Observe the limit values in chapter 9.
  - Intended use includes following the manual and the service intervals, operating within the specified conditions, and using trained personnel only.
- 2.2 Organizational measures:
  - Wear no loose clothing, scarves, ties or jewellery. Tie back long hair.
  - Gloves only for sharp tools and for rough or hot parts.
  - Wear safety glasses for set-up with the door open (spindle running, air blowing, spray gun) and safety shoes.
  - No alcohol or drugs. Keep the floor clean.
  - No add-on equipment or conversions without Mikron's **written approval**. Use original Mikron parts only.
- 2.3 Product-specific dangers:
  - High accelerations and feeds.
  - Modes: "automatic", "special mode set-up" and "special mode extended set-up". Extended set-up is key-switch protected and for specially trained staff only; remove and store the key afterwards.
  - Never exceed the tool or holder maximum speed.
  - Transport/installation rules: local power supplier connection rules, national accident regulations, DIN 32541, pressure/gas accumulator regulations.
- Residual risks during maintenance at standstill: prestressed spring packages, high-voltage capacitors, pressurised hydraulic pipes.
- Cabin windows age: two window types exist; replace the panes at the intervals in 5.3.7.
- 2.4 Electrical: work only by a qualified specialist; live work needs a second person. Disconnect the machine and options and secure them against reconnection.
- 2.4 Hydraulics: switch off and secure, system depressurised. Rules: TRB 003, 004, 005 (pressure accumulators); ZH 1/400.
- 2.4 Pneumatics: shut down and secure via the main valve on the pneumatic panel.
- 2.4 Cooling system: see the cooling aggregate manual.
- 2.5 Environment: collect used fluids in suitable containers and dispose of them properly.
- Warning: guards, covers and safety devices must never be removed, bridged or modified.
- Warning: never stay inside the protection enclosure while the machine works.
- Warning: unreplaced window panes lose their resistance to flying parts.
- Warning: wait for capacitor discharge before electrical work, at least 5 min (or per the component maker).
- Warning: neglect of these rules can cause crushing, shearing, pulling-in, cutting or winding injuries, or death.

## 3. Design and function (p. 10-26)

### 3.1 Brief description (p. 10-11)

- Double-column portal construction on a monoblock **polymer-concrete** machine base (MB).
- Frictionless roller bearing guides. Maximum workpiece weight **200 kg** on the table side.
- Ribbed cast machine table with integrated guide-carriage surfaces and ballscrew support. Pallet clamping device integrated in the table (customer's choice).
- Spindle saddle: ribbed box-type cast iron.
- Feed axes: **acceleration 10 m/s² in all axes**. AC servomotors with recirculating ball screws and high-resolution linear scales on all linear axes.
- Spindle: high-frequency type, ceramic bearings, mist lubrication, liquid cooled by a cooling unit.
- 3.1.1 Intended for HSM with cutting speeds 5-10 times those of conventional milling.
- Fig. (p. 10): photo of the HSM 400U with pendant operating panel.

### 3.2.1 Axes drives (p. 13-15)

- Fig. legend:
  1. Ball screw, X
  2. Drive motor, X
  3. Ball screw, Y
  4. Drive motor, Y
  5. Ball screw, Z
  6. Drive motor, Z
- Layout per figures: the Y motor sits at the left end of the horizontal beam screw. The Z motor is on top of the spindle slide. The X motor is low on the side of the base, driving the table-side screw.
- Ball guideways are lifetime grease lubricated, with grease pallets on the left and right of the guide shoes.
- Warning: for short-stroke oscillating motion (< 100 mm), program a lubrication stroke of at least 130 mm every 10 min, or ballscrew and rail life drops.
- Fig. (p. 14): items 1 and 2 on the tilting table are not labelled in the legend [unreadable/absent].
- Fig. (p. 15) HSM 400U rotary axes:
  - **C-axis = rotary axis** (table plate).
  - **B-axis = swivel axis** (trunnion).
  - The manual names them **B and C**, not A/C.

### 3.2.2 Spindle unit (SPU) (p. 16)

- High-frequency spindle, hybrid ceramic bearings.
- Oil/air lubrication; air is only the carrier.
- Spindle sleeve liquid cooled by the permanently fitted cooling unit (CU).
- Sealing air keeps the bearings clean.
- Air is blown through the taper during tool change. Tool release is **hydraulic**.
- Use only balanced holders rated for max. **60,000 rpm**. Tools must be balanced to **G 2.5 per VDI 2060 / ISO 1940**.
- Spindle details are on the spindle maker's type plate (next to the performance plate, see ch. 9).
- Noise: approx. **75 dB(A)** at 50-80 % load. Ear protection is recommended.
- Fig.: cutaway photo of a motor spindle.
- Warning: never exceed the tool maker's maximum speed; flying parts can kill.

### 3.2.3 Longitudinal measuring system (p. 17)

- Heidenhain incremental linear scales: photoelectric scanning of a DIADUR glass grating with silicon photocells, sinusoidal output. **Distance-coded (interval-coded) reference marks.**
- Arrangement:

| Measuring device | Axis | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Linear scale | X | on the bearing blocks |
| Linear scale | Y | on the machine base (MB) |
| Linear scale | Z | on the cross slide |
| Incremental shaft encoder | Tool changer (TC) | in the drive motor |
| Incremental shaft encoder | X, Y, Z | in the drive motor |
| Incremental shaft encoder | 4th and 5th axis | in the drive motor |
| Incremental shaft encoder | Workpiece changer (WPC) | on the gearbox of the rotary axis |
| Absolute shaft encoder | B-axis | on the rotor |
| Absolute shaft encoder | C-axis | on the rotor |

### 3.2.4 Tool changer (TC) (p. 18-19)

- Disk-type magazine, loaded manually, tools hanging cutting edge down.
- The axes pick tools directly from the magazine (pick-up changer).
- The magazine sits in a separate section behind an **automatic door**.
- Capacity: **18, 36 or 68 tools, HSK E40**.
- 3.2.4.1 Components:
  1. Magazine with retention claws
  2. Tool presence sensor, outer radius
  3. Tool presence sensor, inner radius
  4. Reference position sensor
  5. Light barrier sender (tool inserted correctly)
  6. Light barrier receiver
  7. Gearbox
  8. Drive motor
  9. Magazine loading door
  10. Cycle switch for rotating the magazine manually
  11. EMERGENCY STOP key (at the magazine)
- Insert the touch probe with its battery ON/OFF switch facing the operator (see 4.9.3).
- Warning: wear gloves when loading or unloading the magazine or spindle.

### 3.2.5 Workpiece changer (WPC) (p. 20)

- Fully integrated WPC is standard. Magazine holds **7, 20 or 48 pallets** depending on pallet type.

### 3.2.9 Lubrication and hydraulics (HYP) (p. 25)

Three hydraulic circuits plus the MLU:

1. **Central oil lubrication**: consumable (total-loss) oil delivered at intervals through a multi-line system to many points. Automatic, piston pump.
2. **Spindle oil lubrication**: consumable. Spent oil is sucked by a vacuum pump into a separate container (5).
3. **MLU**: oil mist for lubricating and cooling the cut.
4. **Hydraulic unit**: clamps and releases the tool. **System pressure 100 bar; spindle tool retention 70 bar.**
5. **Oil basin**: collects spindle drain oil. Check and empty it regularly, and never pour the oil back into unit (2). Details in ch. 5.

### 3.2.10 Pneumatic equipment (p. 25)

Compressed air tasks:

- blowing out the spindle taper
- spindle sealing air
- overpressure in the linear scales (dirt protection)
- TC clapper (door) and positioning
- lubrication
- pallet monitoring

Air conditioning unit: see ch. 5.

### 3.3 Milling and drilling (p. 26)

- 3.3.1 CNC details are in the separate control manuals.
- 3.3.2 Three synchronous main axes X, Y, Z. The HSM 400U adds **B and C**, with simultaneous 5-axis feed.

### 3.4 Safety and monitoring devices (p. 26)

- 3.4.1 EMERGENCY STOP: triggered by the E-STOP key or by an E-STOP message from the control.
- 3.4.2 Door lock:
  - The cabin door is locked electrically and mechanically during operation (EU / SUVA rules).
  - With the door open, axis motion and spindle rotation are inhibited.
  - Door lock key: see 4.2.4 and 4.3.2.
- 3.4.3 Software limit switches:
  - After referencing, every axis end is limited by CNC software limits set in machine parameters.
  - If one is hit during a program, the machine locks and shows "LIMIT SWITCH X".
  - To recover, move away in handwheel or jog mode.
- 3.4.4 Spindle monitoring switch:
  - An analog sensor in the SPU detects tool unclamped, tool clamped, and clamped without a tool.
  - With no tool: message "20 TOOL CLAMPED INCORRECT". The spindle must not rotate without a tool.
- 3.5 Options: see ch. 8.

## For the retrofit

- **Control and axis naming:**
  - The manual is for **iTNC 530** (A501, Ethernet X25), not iTNC 520. Check which control the machine actually has.
  - The rotary axes are **B (swivel) and C (rotary)**, also called 4th/5th or IV/V axis, not A/C. Check this against the actual machine.
- **Feedback arrangement (key for stmbl):**
  - X/Y/Z: Heidenhain incremental linear glass scales with **distance-coded reference marks** (homing logic must support them).
  - X/Y/Z/4th/5th motors also have incremental shaft encoders in the motor.
  - B and C have an **absolute encoder "on the rotor"**, which suggests direct-drive or torque motors [uncertain].
  - Scales need **purge air** (pneumatic overpressure).
- **Dynamics:**
  - 10 m/s² on all axes, AC servos with ballscrews.
  - Workpiece max 200 kg (relevant for B/C inertia). The handwritten MP4210.47 "workpiece weight" note suggests the iTNC adapts to load [uncertain].
- **Lubrication rule:** for strokes < 100 mm, a ≥ 130 mm lubrication stroke is needed every 10 min. Replicate this in LinuxCNC.
- **Spindle:**
  - HF motor spindle, oil/air lubricated, liquid cooled, hydraulic tool release.
  - Analog clamp-state sensor: unclamped / clamped / clamped without tool. The new PLC must read it and inhibit spindle start without a tool.
  - Holders rated 60,000 rpm. The original spindle drive stays, but its enable and interlocks must be rewired.
- **Hydraulics:** 100 bar system, 70 bar tool retention. Unit in the ELC; it switches tool clamp/unclamp.
- **Pneumatics the control switches:**
  - taper blow-out
  - spindle sealing air
  - scale purge
  - TC clapper door and positioning
  - lubrication
  - pallet monitoring
- **Lubrication units:** central oil (piston pump) for ballscrews and guideways; spindle oil-mist unit with vacuum return; MLU. The guideways themselves are lifetime-greased.
- **Tool changer:**
  - Pick-up disk magazine (18/36/68 × HSK E40), own drive motor and gearbox, incremental encoder in the motor.
  - Sensors: inner and outer tool presence, reference position, tool-inserted light barrier.
  - Inputs: manual rotate switch, own E-STOP key.
  - Automatic TC door.
- **WPC:** linear axis plus lifting axis, rotary magazine with an encoder on the gearbox. Pallet chuck clamp in the table.
- **Safety:**
  - E-STOP keys on the panel, the TC magazine and the optional handwheel.
  - Door locked electrically and mechanically (EU/SUVA); open door inhibits axes and spindle.
  - Software limits are in machine parameters.
- **Cooling:** the ELC heat exchangers share the spindle water circuit, so the cooling unit must run whenever the cabinet is powered.
- **Interfaces:** handwheel HR130 on the panel, optional mobile handwheel, RS-232, 230 V socket.
- **Machine parameters noted on the cover:** MP4210.47 (workpiece weight) and MP4230.9 (tool change point Z). Record their values before removing the iTNC.
