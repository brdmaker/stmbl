### 5.3.3 Guideways of the linear axes

X, Y, Z guides are maintenance-free. WPC linear guides must be greased regularly (5.3.1, 5.3.2).

### 5.3.4 Replacement of the telescopic covers

1. Remove all screws left and right of the protection cover.
2. Lift the cover off.
3. Install the new cover and screw it on.

### 5.3.5 Tool changer (TC)

- Holds 18, 36 or 72 tools depending on type; tool type HSK E 40. Shown: TC68 (HSK-40), outer gripper and inner gripper.
- Warning: remove all tools from the disk magazine before TC service (cut/puncture risk).
- Warning: wear protective gloves when loading/unloading the disk magazine.
- Parts (fig.): 1 angular drive, 2 magazine drive motor, 3 gear drive, 4 magazine pocket, 5 tool present sensor, 6 light barrier "tool inserted correctly", 7 pneumatic cylinder of work-area door.
- Function: TC is behind the X-axis protection cover, accessible via the side maintenance/loading door. Magazine driven by motor (2) via angular drive (1) and gear drive (3). Tool presence and length checked by contactless switches (5). Work-area door opened/closed by pneumatic cylinder (7). A magazine reference point switch initialises magazine position at start-up. Light barrier (6) checks tools snapped into grippers.
- Maintenance: angular gear grease-lubricated, maintenance-free. Clean gear drive (3) and magazine crown gear weekly, lightly lubricate with universal grease. Switches (5) factory-adjusted; further adjustment only by Mikron service.

### 5.3.6 Chip removal

- Variant 1, chip box: remove chips by shovel or industrial vacuum.
- Variant 2, chip removal unit: longitudinal conveyor (1) and cross conveyor (2) to chip trolley or collector basin (3); trolley/basin not supplied ex works.

### 5.3.7 Cabin (CAB)

- Window panes degrade from coolant/oil mist; replacement is mandatory:
  - Variant 1, polycarbonate panes (item 205.10/1): replace every 2 years.
  - Variant 2, scratchproof glass/polycarbonate sandwich (item 205.10/3): replace every 8 years.
- Caution: clean windows only with soap water or household detergent without ammonia; no aggressive chemicals or abrasives.

## 5.4 Electrical maintenance work

- Observe 5.1. Electrical work only by a qualified electrician per regulations.
- Only original spare parts per the Mikron electro-documentation equipment list.
- Wiring diagrams are binding; must be studied; no changes to them.
- Modifying the electrical installation, especially safety functions, is forbidden; manufacturer not liable otherwise.
- Faults reported by others must be fixed immediately.

### 5.4.4 Replacing the buffer batteries

- Battery powers TNC RAM while control is off. Replace when TNC shows "EXCHANGE BUFFER BATTERY". An energy accumulator bridges up to 24 h during the change.
- Fig.: 1 front panel with plugged connections, 2 buffer battery with bracket, 3 hard disk.
- Procedure:
  1. Switch off at main switch Q111 and secure.
  2. Unplug all connectors on the front panel (1) and take the TNC out of the cabinet; avoid shocks, especially to the hard disk (3).
  3. Pull the battery sideways out of its bracket (rear of the unit) and replace.
- Battery: 1 lithium CR 2450N (Renata), Id.-No. 315 878-01.
- Warning: main switch Q111 off and secured; risk of death by electrocution.
- Caution: do not touch internal parts; ESD can destroy components.

### 5.4.6 Data interfaces

- Standard V.24/RS-232-C interface, on the rear of the operating panel (see 3.2).
- RS-232C wiring (TNC X27 -> panel adapter -> PC): TNC pins 1 GND, 2 RXD, 3 TXD, 4 DTR, 5 Signal GND, 6 DSR, 7 RTS, 8 CTS; the TNC-to-panel cable crosses 2/3, 4/6 and 7/8, so at the panel/PC side: 1 GND, 2 TXD, 3 RXD, 4 DSR, 5 Signal, 6 DTR, 7 CTS, 8 RTS; panel to PC is 1:1 (pins 1-9).
- Note: because of the transposed cable, X27 on the control has a different pin assignment than the panel connector.

### 5.4.7 RS-422/V.11 data interface

- LE X22 (as printed; table above says X28) -> cable V11/LE item 250 478 -> V.11 adapter block Id.-Nr. 249 819 01 -> cable for purchased appliances ("Fremdgeräte"). 1:1 pins: 1 shield, 2 RXD, 3 CTS, 4 TXD, 5 RTS, 6 DSR, 7 DTR, 8 GND, 9 /RXD, 10 /CTS, 11 /TXD, 12 /RTS, 13 /DSR, 14 /DTR.
- At 38400 baud, up to 1 km cable. Same pin assignment on logic unit and operating panel.

### 5.4.8 Service concept iTNC 530

- Hard disk up to 2 GB.
- Warning: user must back up data regularly, especially before control exchange or software update; Mikron not liable; if not done before service visit, backup time is invoiced.
- Commissioning data is stored at Mikron in the machine file, and in the cabinet folder "BACKUP - iTNC 530" on floppy disks; allows restoring delivery state.
- A hard copy of machine parameters and non-linear axis error compensation tables is also available.
- Backup via a data transfer program over serial or Ethernet; full disk backup can take long, depending on PC.

## 5.5 Waste disposal

- Lubricants: waste-oil collection. Warning: wrong disposal damages environment.
- Cooling circuit coolant: follow supplier instructions (MOTOREX Coolant-F).
- Cooling emulsion: per emulsion maker.
- Polymer concrete EPUCRET (machine base): per European waste catalogue (Germany since 1 Jan 1999) and LAGA guideline; may go to category 1 rubble sites; shredded material usable under LAGA category 1.2 (roads, industrial construction, site seals, green areas).
- Metal: scrap collection. Electrical parts (sensors, cables, motors, wires): electrical waste collection.
- Note: follow local regulations. Warning: non-compliance can harm groundwater, air, fauna, flora.

- Note: check for damage on receipt; note "reception of goods with acceptance reservations" on delivery papers and inform the Mikron subsidiary.

- Warning: rest the machine on solid supports (double squared timber 200 x 100 or similar) when fitting the pads.
- Warning: crane only with the supplied lifting ropes; read lifting instructions first.

## For the retrofit

- E-STOP: 3 E-STOP keys plus door locking switches; schedule requires testing them every 40 h and after maintenance, at reference run. The new safety chain must keep these.
- LE iTNC 530 interface map tells which signals leave the old control: X15-X20 1 Vss ("axes position", likely motor encoders [uncertain]), X1-X5 1 Vss position scales, X51-X60 PWM to Heidenhain power modules, X150 axis drive release 1-6, X34 "control ready" 24 V input, X30 24 V spindle reference, X41/X42/X47 PLC I/O (PL 4xxB extension), X23 handwheel, X12/X13 TS/TT 130 probes, X8/X9 analogue outputs (likely spindle drive setpoint [uncertain]). With the original spindle drive kept, its setpoint/enable/reference wiring at X8/X9/X30/X150 must be traced in the electrical diagrams.
- This table lists all encoder inputs as 1 Vss sin/cos (EnDat 2.1 encoders also carry 1 Vss tracks). The stmbl/LinuxCNC side therefore needs sin/cos or EnDat 2.1 input. Check this against the motor and scale labels.
- Back up before removing the TNC: machine parameters and non-linear axis error compensation tables (hard copy and "BACKUP - iTNC 530" floppies exist); these hold axis/kinematics data for the retrofit.
- Z axis uses a toothed belt from the feed motor and can drop if the belt fails: the Z motor brake and brake control must be kept and the belt replaced yearly.
- Tool changer: magazine motor with angular and gear drive, magazine reference switch, tool present/length contactless switches, "tool inserted correctly" light barrier, pneumatic work-area door (Y601/Y602); 18/36/72 pockets, HSK E 40. All must be handled in the new PLC logic.
- Hydraulic oil Mobil DTE 24 (VG 32), central lube Esso Febis K 68; NLGI 2 grease for TC door and WPC.
- RS-232/RS-422 interfaces are irrelevant after the retrofit apart from the old-control backup.
