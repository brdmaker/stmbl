## 2.2 Performance diagram main drive 202 Nm

(Page 2-4)

Motor data (S1):
- P<sub>N</sub> = 18.0 kW
- M<sub>N</sub> = 202 Nm
- n<sub>N</sub> = 850 rpm

n = 25 - 4000 rpm (next to a small sketch of the motor with shaft at both ends).

Figure: log-log diagram, power P [kW] (left axis, 0.112 ... 28.0, marked "18,0") and torque Md [Nm] (right axis, 3.55 ... 900, marked "202") over speed n [min⁻¹] (16 ... 5000, with "850 - 800" [uncertain] marked at the corner). Legend: P = solid black line, Md = double (hollow) line. Power rises linearly from about 25 min⁻¹ to 18.0 kW at 850 min⁻¹ and stays constant to 4000; torque is constant at 202 Nm from 25 to 850 min⁻¹ and then falls (constant power) to about 45 Nm at 4000 min⁻¹.

## For the retrofit

- Axes per side: X1/X2 (cross slide, 45° inclined, stroke 232 mm), Z1 (tool slide longitudinal, 380 mm) on the left side; on the right side the spindle motor/headstock moves (Z2 "spindle motor longitudinal stroke" 1165 mm, "headstock stroke" HZ2 1165) and the table also gives HZA "Stroke Z2" 380 (p. 2-3, 2-7). The moving right headstock is what makes part transfer between the spindles possible; max. spindle spacing 1361 mm.
- Machine zero points M1, M2 at the spindle noses; A1 = 123.5, A2 = 503.5, F = 520 (spindle axis to tool holder), B = 22, LW = 130 (p. 2-7).
- Feed motors: Indramat MAC 93 A (X, 2000 rpm, 9.2 Nm S1, 26 Nm peak) and MAC 93 B (Z, 3000 rpm, 14.5 Nm S1, 38.3 Nm peak); direct 1:1 to ball screws 32 x 10 (X) and 40 x 10 (Z); rapid 16 m/min X, 24 m/min Z; feed force 4000 / 8000 (N or daN, inconsistent) (p. 2-3, 2-8). 10 mm pitch at 1:1 means rapid = motor max speed x 10 mm (X 2000 rpm = 20 m/min ceiling, Z 3000 rpm = 30 m/min).
- Spindles: two integrated AC spindle motors, 18 kW / 202 Nm S1, 25-4000 rpm, base (kink) speed 850 rpm; spindle nose 170 h5 flat flange, bore 65 mm, bar 51 mm (p. 2-3, 2-4).
- C axis (option): 100 rpm, 202 Nm, done by the main spindle motor (p. 2-3).
- Turret: 12 stations per side (2 x 12), VDI 40 (DIN 69880) shanks, 30° per station, 0.2 s per station, "autom. indexing with directional logics" (shortest path); optional driven tools on all 12 stations, max. 3000 rpm, 5.3 kW / 24 Nm at S6 36 %, 2.5 kW / 14.5 Nm at S1, collet max. 16 mm (p. 2-3, 2-5). Tool circle 620 mm, swing circle 720 mm (p. 2-6).
- Chuck 200 mm, max. turning dia. 230 mm, chuck part length 200 mm (p. 2-3).
- Machine 5200 x 2700 x 1900 mm, 9500 kg; noise 77 dB(A) (p. 2-3).
