## 4.1 Brief instructions for feeders

(Page 4-3)

These "brief instructions for feeders" should be used by trained staff as notes.

### 4.1.1 Starting the machine

**Prerequisites:**

- The machine is set up
- The first workpiece is correctly machined
- The control system is in the `MACHINE` / `AUTOMATIC` operating mode

- **Select selector switch AG1 or AG2, dependent on parallel or full machining**
- **Carefully insert the part (check the position of the chuck jaw)**
- **Close chuck with the right hand foot switch (at least 2 seconds)**
- **Close the protection hood(s)**
  - Wait until the green lamp(s) in the button(s) is (are) cancelled and the screen releases appear
    (Key symbol: two side-by-side buttons, each with a square lamp, arrows pointing inwards towards a centre symbol: the protection hood buttons for working space 1 and 2.)
- **Start the program with key** (Key symbol: cycle start, a rectangle with circular arrow and "I".)

✓ The workpiece is now being machined.

For machines with **automatic protection hoods** use these buttons to select the working space to be closed. The left hand button closes the working space 1, the right hand button closes the working space 2. (Same two-button symbol.)

The required protection hood(s) is (are) closed by pressing the mushroom buttons located at the side of the operating panel and the cycle is automatically started when this is preset by a parameter.

It is important that the mushroom buttons are simultaneously pressed and kept depressed until the door(s) is (are) closed.

### 4.1.2 Stopping the machine

(Page 4-4. Margin symbol: hand/stop pictogram with "!".)

Stop the machine in the event of a tool breakage or cutting problems:

- **Select selector switch AG1 or AG2, dependent on parallel or full machining**
- and cycle stop button (Key symbol: rectangle with circular arrow and "O".)
- and notify the setter.

### 4.1.3 Changing the workpiece

- **When the unit group AG 1 or AG 2 or the machine is stationary, the blue button(s) is (are) illuminated.**

  Press the left or right hand button, the corresponding protection hood will be unlocked. In unsplit operating mode it is sufficient to press both buttons in order to unlock both protection hoods. (Key symbol: the two hood buttons with arrows pointing outwards.)

- **Open protection hood(s) and release button(s)**
- **Take hold of part (if necessary with crane)**
- **Open chuck with left hand foot switch and take out part**
- **Remove chips and dirt from the chuck and insert a new workpiece**

By pressing this (these) button(s) the protection hood(s) is (are) opened in accordance with the pressed button(s) on machines with **automatic protection hood**, in as much as this function is not programmed with cycle end.

## For the retrofit

- The two sides are "unit groups" `AG1` and `AG2`, chosen with a selector switch; operation is either "parallel machining" (each side its own part) or "full machining" (part machined on both sides, i.e. with transfer) (p. 4-3, 4-4). An "unsplit operating mode" treats both as one machine (p. 4-4).
- Chuck clamping by foot switches: right foot switch closes the chuck (must be held at least 2 s), left foot switch opens it (p. 4-3, 4-4).
- Protection hoods (doors) per working space: two illuminated buttons on the panel (left = working space 1, right = working space 2); green lamp lit until the hood is closed/locked, then screen release appears; blue lamp lit when the unit group or machine is stationary, pressing it unlocks that hood; in unsplit mode both buttons unlock both hoods (p. 4-3, 4-4). Door locking with standstill interlock is required.
- Optional automatic (powered) protection hoods: close by two-hand operation, both mushroom buttons at the side of the panel held until the door is closed; cycle may auto-start after closing if set by a parameter; hood may open automatically at cycle end if programmed (p. 4-3, 4-4).
- Cycle start / cycle stop keys act on the selected unit group (p. 4-3, 4-4).
