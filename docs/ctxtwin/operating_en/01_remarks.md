## 1.1 Who are these instructions intended for

(Page 1-3)

The Gildemeister CNC lathe CTX twin is a robust, high capacity machine with a high standard of technology, which requires careful operation. For this reason it may only be operated by trained personnel.

The required knowledge can, amongst others, be obtained in special Gildemeister training courses.

Furthermore, the operator should be familiar with the operation of conventional lathes and the technological basics of chip removal (e.g. cutting material, materials, cutting values, cooling etc.). Construction and method of operation of CNC lathes and CNC controls should also be known (e.g. setting, tool correction, program selection etc.).

Our prerequisite is that the machine is only operated and maintained by trained personnel. Exception being simple, daily repetitive work such as checking the oil level or the repeated insertion of identical workpieces.

## 1.2 What do these instructions contain

These instructions describe the daily operating and maintenance instructions of the machine and its controls.

For operators who are not yet fully familiar with the machine, the chapter 5, "Operating the machine and its controls" and chapter 6 "Setting" are particularly important.

For simple, daily repetitive routine work, which can also performed by instructed assistant labour, please refer to chapter 4 "Brief instructions for feeders".

We hope that these instructions will be of assistance during the learning period and for any problems which may be encountered.

## 1.3 What is _not_ contained in these instructions?

(Page 1-4)

**Programming**
The programming, as such, is described in the programming manual.

**Technical details**
Technical details and repair instructions can be found in the technical service instructions.

## 1.4 Notes

For reasons of clarity these operating instructions do not contain detailed information for all product variants and cannot take into consideration every conceivable type of machine installation, operation or maintenance.

If you require further information, or if special problems occur, which are not dealt with in detail in the operating instructions, you can obtain the necessary information from your Gildemeister agent.
