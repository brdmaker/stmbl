## 8.1 Maintenance

(Page 8-3)

As with all machines - and we too - even the robust CTX lathes requires regular care and maintenance.

(Margin symbol: pointing hand = Note.)
Please take the following information very seriously and very precisely, and observe the following maintenance plan together with the "Lubrication points" plate on the machine.

## 8.2 Oil levels, checks

Undertake the following work prior to each shift:

- **Check the oil levels of the hydraulic system**

The inspection glass showing the hydraulic oil level is located at the hydraulic cabinet lower section on the machine right-hand side. Ensure that the oil level is above the red mark.

(Margin symbol: warning/caution pictogram "!".)
Under no circumstances switch on the machine when it has insufficient hydraulic oil.
In this case there is a leak in the closed hydraulic system circuit. Contact your service department.

- **Fresh oil lubricant level**

The fresh oil lubrication container is located in the supply cabinet at the rear of the machine. This can be clearly seen through the grating. Ensure that the oil level is above the black mark.

If the container holds insufficient oil top up with the corresponding quantity, see 3.7.2., "lubricant and coolant" on page 3-16.

- **Coolant lubricant filling level**

An inspection glass on the coolant lubricant container of the chip conveyor shows the coolant lubricant level.
The mark in the inspection glass should be covered.

(Margin symbol: warning/caution pictogram "!".)
If required top up using the correct mixing ratio (if necessary, check with a Refractometer in advance).

(Page 8-4)

- **Bar guidance / bar feeder (option) filling level**

Check the oil level of the bar guide and the bar feeder.

- **Cooling unit level**

The coolant container is accommodated in the supply cabinet on the right hand side of the machine, see 5-5.
Ensure that the liquid level is above the mark.

If the container coolant level is too low top up with a corresponding quantity, see 3.7.2 "lubricant and coolant" on page 3-16.

## 8.3 Working area, check and cleaning

- Clean the working space with a hand broom after each shift.
- Grease chuck/collet (using heavy duty grease)

(Margin symbol: pointing hand = Note.)
If damage is found during the following visual checks, please contact your internal service department immediately.

- Check chuck/collet for damage
- Check slideway covers for damage
- Check oil lines for kinks and damage
- Clean operating field, screen and control lights
- Check foot switch for damage, if necessary clean.
- Check for damage and, if necessary, clean protection hood window.

(Margin symbol: warning/caution pictogram "!".)
**Corrosion on slideways**
During an extended down time, e.g. holidays, long weekends etc., slideways might corrode.

- Clean the slideways prior to such downtime.
- Oil the slideways by running the axes to their end position whilst giving additional lubrication pulses with the M49 command.
- Also activate additional lubrication impulses after an extended down time.

## For the retrofit

- Central fresh-oil (total-loss) lubrication for slideways and ball screws: container in the supply cabinet at the rear, dosing elements, leak oil catcher vessel (p. 8-3, 8-5). M49 gives additional lubrication pulses (manual trigger) and is used to oil the slideways while running the axes to their end positions before/after long downtime (p. 8-4). The new control must run periodic lube pulses plus an M49-equivalent and a level monitor.
- Hydraulic unit at the lower right-hand side of the machine with oil level sight glass (red mark), oil filter and temperature; machine must not be switched on with low hydraulic oil (p. 8-3, 8-5) - a low-level/pressure interlock is needed before hydraulic functions (chuck, turret clamp).
- Spindle motor cooling unit (coolant container in the supply cabinet on the right) with level, filter and temperature checks - the integrated spindle motors are liquid cooled (p. 8-4, 8-5).
- Coolant tank is on the chip conveyor (sight glass); chip conveyor; switch cabinet ventilator with filter mat and cooler ("liquefier") (p. 8-3, 8-6).
- Assemblies named: power chuck (chuck, jaws, clamp), tool turret by Sauter, pick-off device, optional bar guide / bar feeder with own oil level, foot switch, protection hood window (p. 8-4 to 8-6).
