## For the retrofit

- Chuck: foot switch (3-5, 5-20), chuck actuation and chucking pressure setting (5-20), chucking stroke/pressure/mode/chuck type (6-11 to 6-17), two-pressure chucking device (6-36), hollow clamping unit (3-7).
- Process/machine items: C axis (3-6, 6-28), driven tools starting (6-29), pick-off device (5-8, 6-32), workpiece ejector (6-34), tool measuring device (6-37), overload clutch (3-6, 6-49), bar feeding/bar loading magazine (6-39 to 6-47), coolant through the turning spindle and two-pressure coolant (3-7), spindle motor coolant (3-17, 8-4), oil mist separator (3-6), "Moving free, position switch" (5-38).
