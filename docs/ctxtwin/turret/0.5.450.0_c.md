## 4. Troubleshooting remedies

### 4.4.4. Dismantling / Fitting Mounting disk group and indexing group (page 41)

Figure: exploded side view along the turret axis, from left to right: coolant ring (14) with fixing screws (13) and sealing ring (12); mounting disk (1) with L ring (11) and its screws (torque T_A1), fixing screws torque T_A2; index plate (2) (with spring-loaded pin below); hexagon wrench (10); housing (3) with control fork (8), detail (a), cover (9) on top; axial bearing (7) between disks (5) and (6); gearwheel (4) at the right (rear).

**Working steps:**
1. Remove cover (9)
2. Unscrew coolant ring (14).
3. Undo control fork (8) with hexagon wrench.
4. Undo fixing screws (13) and dismantle mounting disk (1) without tilting it.
5. Unscrew L ring (11).
6. Pull out index plate (2).
7. Remove gearwheel (4) from the rear.

**When reinstalling:** (warning triangle)
- Sealing rings (12) should only be used once.
- Fit seals in correct position and watch out for damage.
- Position L ring (11) correctly with respect to the housing (3) and fasten it. Screw tightening torque T_A1 in accordance with Table 8.
- Ensure that the axial bearing (7) is correctly located with respect to disks (5 and 6).
- Mounting disk (1) and gearwheel (4) can be fitted only in a certain position; tighten fixing screw with screw tightening torque T_A2 in accordance with Table 8.
- Apply screw locking fluid (e.g. LOCTITE 221) to the contol [sic] fork (8, a)

| 16 | M6: 17 Nm | M8: 41 Nm |
| 20 | M8: 41 Nm | M10: 83 Nm |
| 25 | M10: 83 Nm | M12: 145 Nm |

### 4.5. Basic setting (page 42)

Figure: side view of the turret: tool disk (1), marking (2), coolant ring (3), coupling sleeve with centre-tooth marking (4) (View X: splined coupling sleeve with marked tooth), control fork (5) visible in a housing window with positions "Unlocked" / "locked" marked above it, gearwheel (6), plug (7) with hexagon wrench and torque T_A, cover (8) with two proximity switches (detail at upper right), flat material (9) shown separately, approx. 20 mm wide and 8 mm thick ("~20", "8"), marks "E" on the housing. View Y: gearwheel (6) meshing with gear (10).

**Working steps:**
1. Install disk-type tool turret hydraulically. Reduce hydraulic pressure to 10 bar for the purposes of setting.
2. Install disk-type tool turret electrically, without AC servomotor.
3. Unlock hydraulically (pressure approx. 10 bar). If not possible via control, relocate connector on hydraulic valve.
4. Turn tool disk by hand approximately to position 1 (±1.5°).

(Page 43)

5. Lock hydraulically.
6. Align tool disk (1) in accordance with description (see 2.5.5.).
7. Mark exactly the rotational position of tool disk (1) and coolant ring (3).
8. Turn gearwheel (6) by hand until pin and marking (2) line up.
9. Unlock hydraulically, place flat material (9) - approx. 8 mm thick - between control fork (5) and housing.
10. Lock hydraulically; marking on tool disk (1) must be in line with that of the coolant ring (3).
11. Pull plug (7), undo screws on gearwheel (6) with hexagon wrench.
12. Turn coupling sleeve until marking - centre tooth - is in line with the turret axis.
13. Tighten screws on gearwheel (6).
14. Carefully place straight spindle head in working position and tighten.
15. Unlock tool turret hydraulically. Do **not** remove flat material (9). Centrally align spindle head locking device, mark rotational position with a pencil.
16. Lock hydraulically and observe the spindle head while doing so. When locking, the rotational position at the spindle head should be maintained.
    If necessary, correct as follows:
    Undo screws on gearwheel (6), turn spindle head to marking and tighten screws on gearwheel with T_A in accordance with Table 9 below.
17. Fit plug (7).
18. Fit AC servomotor with ratchetting clutch in correct position and install electrically.
19. Hydraulically unlock disk-type tool turret, remove flat material (9), fit cover (8) and limit switch.
20. Determination of reference point as described under 2.5.6. above.

Table 9: Screw tightening torques for basic setting pt.16

| Size | 12 | 16 | 20 | 25 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tightening torque T_A | 1.5 Nm | 4.9 Nm | 4.9 Nm | (empty) |

## For the retrofit

- **Locking and unlocking, hydraulic.**
  - These pages name the locking parts: control fork (8 or 5), index plate (2), mounting disk (1), L ring (11) and sliding blocks (27, 11 pcs), plus locking-spool seals 4, 5 and 23-26 (pages 41, 42, 52-53).
  - The control fork moves between the "Unlocked" and "locked" positions marked on the housing (page 42).
  - For setting and service, lock and unlock with about 10 bar (page 42).
  - If the control cannot unlock it, move the connector on the hydraulic valve (page 42). This means the valve solenoids are plugged on, so the new control must drive them (`Y1.1a`/`Y1.1b`, part a page 20).
- **Position and lock sensing.** All switches are Balluff-type inductive proximity switches:
  - Bearing plate (motor end): 2 × BES 516-324-E0-K-1, Id 004 157 (page 50).
  - Limit switches beside the control fork: 2 × BES 516-324-E4-C-PU, Id 076 749 (page 51). These are presumably `S4`/`S5`, disengaged/engaged (compare part a page 18) [inference].
  - Indexing unit: 4 × BES 516-324-EO-K-1 with Pg9 glands (page 53).
  - No coded (binary) position sensing is shown. Position comes from the servo encoder and the reference switch (part a).
  - Switch functions and wiring are in part b (2.7, 4.2), not supplied.
- **Indexing drive.** The AC servomotor is coupled through a ratchetting clutch, GRO 450.725.OD24.6885/1, Id 081 593 (page 50). The motor-flange O-ring is listed for a Siemens or an Indramat motor (page 50). After a collision while the turret is swivelled, the clutch can be disengaged and must be re-engaged as described in section 4.3 (part b) (page 44). The control should treat a following error or missing motion during an index as a possible clutch slip.
- **Alignment of motor and disk.** The servomotor is fitted in a fixed position: the pin and the marking must line up (pages 43, 14). After the basic setting, the reference point must be determined again as in 2.5.6 (page 43, step 20).
- **Driven-tool coupling.** The coupling sleeve is centred on the centre tooth (pages 42-43). The spindle head must keep its rotational position while the turret locks (page 43, step 16), so the tool drive probably has to stop in an oriented position before the turret indexes or locks [inference]. If the tool drive no longer engages, suspect a damaged coupling sleeve (page 44). Driven heads have spring-loaded drive ends and a locking device (pages 54-55).
- **Not on these pages:** shortest-path direction logic, timing, valve/switch wiring and tool-drive motor data.
