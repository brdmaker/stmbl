The housing (2) is fastened to the machine.
The holding disk (1) shuts the housing (2) at the front. It holds the tool disk (7).

The coolant connection (3) allows coolant to flow directly into the tool disk (7).

### 1.2. Changing TOOLHOLDERS (page 4)

Figure: left, side view of a tool station with thrust piece (1) and tightening torque arrow "T_Amax"; right, front view of the 12-sided tool disk with a toolholder (4) with adjusting screws (3) at the lower right, a driven spindle head (2) with drill below, a closing plug (6) at the upper left, sealing ring (5) shown on a holder at the right (arrow into the disk). Arrow at top labelled "Swarf / Coolant". A dashed outline indicates a holder being removed diagonally.

**Working steps:**
1. Check the sealing rings (5) on the new toolholder (4) [are they there and undamaged?].
2. Unscrew the thrust pieces (1).
3. Remove the toolholder (4) that is to be replaced or closing plugs (6).
4. Introduce the new holder and secure it. [Note the detent positions of the spindle heads (2), see below.]
5. Align holder to centre of rotation using adjusting screws (3). [Necessary with adapter 90°]
6. Tightly screw down the thrust piece (1) - note the tightening torques [Table 1].

**To avoid functional problems due to dirt:** (warning triangle)
**If possible, change the toolholder in a horizontal attitude !**
**Clean mating surfaces on the tool disk and toolholder.**
**Fill all locating holes, using closing plugs where necessary.**

Suitable holders and plugs in accordance with DIN 69 880 Part 1, Issue Juli 1990 !

### 2.4. Description of function (page 11)

Figure: schematic section of the turret. Left: tool disk with a driven spindle head; the tool-drive shaft runs through the centre with a bevel gear to the spindle head; spool Z2 (vertical, double arrow) at the tool disk slides the tool-drive coupling. Middle: locking spool Z1 (annular piston at the holding disk, top and bottom). Right: gearbox with spur-wheel gear (5) and spool Z3 (horizontal double arrow) that couples the swivel drive; at the far right the AC servomotor (motor symbol) with the rotary encoder (pulse symbol "⎍").

**Unlocking**
of the tool disk with locking spool Z1
Disengaging of the tool drive with spool Z2
Engaging of the swivel drive with spool Z3

**Swivelling**
of the tool disk via spur-wheel gear (5)
with AC servomotor, positioning via rotary encoder

**Locking**
of the tool disk with locking spool Z1
Engaging of the tool drive with spool Z2
Disengaging of the swivel drive with spool Z3

The clutch and locking spools operate at the same time.

### 2.5. Mounting on the machine

#### 2.5.1. Disk-Type Tool Turret - MOUNTING ON THE MACHINE (page 12)

Figure: turret foot (1) on intermediate plate (4) on the machine slide contact surface (3); pivot-point bushing (2) at left; bolt with washer (5) at right with note "play increased"; machine axis of rotation (6); flatness tolerance "▱ 0,01/100" on the intermediate plate.

**Working steps:**
1. Determine the center height (1) between contact surface of the machine (3) and axis of rotation of the machine (6).
2. Subtract from this the center height of the tool turret.
3. Adjust intermediate plate (4) to suit the obtained dimension.
4. Mount tool turret together with intermediate plate (4) and pivot-point bushing (2) and fasten with bolts and washers (5) supplied.
   **Note:** Hole for pivot-point bushing (2) must already exist in the slide in the required position and quality.
5. Electrical connections in accordance with the connection diagram (2.7.1.). [in part b, not supplied]
6. Hydraulics connections in accordance with hydraulics circuit diagram (2.6.1.).

#### 2.5.2. Fitting the TOOL DISK (if not already fitted) (page 13)

Figure: left, section through tool disk (1), sealing ring (3), centre area (2), teeth (9); right, front view of the toothed coupling ring with blocks (7), teeth (8), bushing (4), grub screw (5), coupling sleeve (6) at "Working position"; detail below: tooth/tooth gap (10).

**Working steps:** (warning triangle)
1. **Clearly mark the rotational position of the teeth** (tooth gap to tooth, items 8 and 10) **on both parts**
2. Remove coupling sleeve (6), to that:
   Loosen grub screw (5)
   Withdraw the coupling sleeve (6)
   Remove the bushing (4)
3. Put the blocks (7) into their correct angular positions
4. Check the sealing ring (3) (is it in place and undamaged?)
5. Fit tool disk (1) in the correct angular position
6. Slightly slacken the screw in the working position to allow the tool disk to be aligned later. It is covered by the coupling sleeve.
7. Push coupling sleeve **in marked rotational position** into teeth, making sure that bushing (4) is located correctly
8. Apply locking fluid (such as LOCTITE 221) to the grub screws (5) and tighten.

#### 2.5.3. Fitting the AC MOTOR (page 14)

Figure: left, end view of the bearing plate with marking (10) and pin (8) in the pinion bore; right, section: motor shaft (1) with feather key (2), ratchetting clutch (3), screw (4), pinion (5), bearing plate (6), slot (7) in clutch, pin (8), sealing ring (9), marking (10), item (11) [part not named on page].

**Working steps:**
1. Check the height of the feather key (2) on the motor shaft (1). If necessary replace or adapt feather key (according to motor manufacturer).
2. Mount ratchetting clutch (3) on motor shaft, secure screw (4) with suitable locking fluid (e.g. LOCTITE 221).
3. **Check:** (warning triangle)
   Are marking (10) on bearing plate (6) and pin (8) in pinion (5) in line and is slot (7) in clutch (3) in line with the pin? Is the sealing ring (9) in place?
4. Mount AC motor with clutch and make sure that slot and pin engage!

(Page 19) **Working steps:**
1. Hydraulics: 10 bar
2. Lock disk-type tool turret in position 1.
3. Put marking between tool disk and coolant ring.
4. Rotate coupling sleeve until marking points to turret axis.
5. Unlock tool turret hydraulically, S5 switched on. [sic; by the legend on page 18 S4 = disengaged, S5 = engaged]
6. By means of AC motor, rotate tool disk clockwise (as viewed on the tool disk) until the reference point switch is switched off and then on again.
7. Rotate tool disk further until the zero pulse is detected at the rotary encoder. The reference point offset is determined from this zero pulse.
8. Rotate tool disk further until the two markings on the tool disk and on the coolant ring are positioned exactly one above the other.
9. Check the function with spindle heads in place.
10. Increase pressure: Hydraulics approx. 50 bar.

## For the retrofit

- **Indexing drive: electric.** "Disk-Type Tool Turret with electrical drive", 0.5.450.020 (p. 1). The tool disk is swivelled by an AC servomotor through a spur-wheel gear (5), "positioning via rotary encoder" (p. 11). No mechanical position code is described: the angular position comes from the motor encoder plus a reference switch (p. 18-19). The motor is coupled through a ratchetting (overload) clutch (p. 14). The clutch limits drive torque to T_max 13/22/40/80 Nm for sizes 12/16/20/25 (p. 8).
- **Hydraulic lock and couplings, one valve.** A single 4/2 valve with solenoids `Y1.1a`/`Y1.1b` drives three spools at the same time (p. 11, 20):
  - `Y1.1a` = 1: Z1 locks the disk, Z3 disengages the swivel drive and Z2 engages the tool drive.
  - `Y1.1b` = 1: unlock, swivel drive engaged, tool drive disengaged.
  - The disk does not move axially when it locks or unlocks (p. 3).
  - These pages do not name the locking element. The figures show a toothed coupling ring and a coupling sleeve (p. 13).
- **Hydraulics.** Operating pressure is 50 bar; use 10 bar for setting and referencing (p. 8, 19-20). The power unit is a 4 l/min pump with a 60 bar relief valve, a 2.5 l accumulator, a 50 bar reducing valve and a 20 µm filter (p. 20). One indexing cycle needs about 34-35 cm³ (size 16) or 53-54 cm³ (size 20) (p. 20).
- **Sensors.**
  - `S4` = tool disk disengaged (unlocked).
  - `S5` = tool disk engaged (locked).
  - `S1` = reference point cam.
  - Rotary encoder zero pulse (p. 18).
  - The switches are described in part b (2.7) and section 4.2, which were not supplied.
- **Homing** (p. 19): unlock, then turn the disk clockwise as seen from the disk until `S1` goes off and on again. Continue to the encoder zero pulse; this gives the reference offset. Position 1 is aligned with the markings.
- **Times and rate** (p. 8):
  - Release 0.14-0.22 s and lock 0.14-0.22 s.
  - 30° (one station) 0.12-0.20 s including acceleration; 180° 0.37-0.61 s.
  - Maximum 18/15/12/10 indexes per minute.
  - These pages do not specify shortest-path direction logic.
- **Inertia, stations and coolant.** There are 12 stations. Permitted J is 1.25/2.5/5/10 kgm² (p. 8). Coolant may be 7 bar constant, or 14 bar only if it is cut out while swivelling (p. 8).
- **Driven tools** (p. 10-11):
  - Up to 4000/4000/3000/3000 rpm and 10/16/25/50 Nm.
  - The tool drive is engaged by Z2 only while the disk is locked.
  - The source of the tool-drive power is not described in part a.
  - Do not change tools in the working position (p. 5).
