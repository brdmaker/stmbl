## For the retrofit

Pointers from the index to operator-panel and machine functions the new control must reproduce (pages as listed above):

- Two-side handling: `Aggregat 1+2` (unit 1+2) and `Wahlschalter AG` (unit-group selector switch) p. 5-10; `Schlitten umschalten` (switch over slide) and `Spindel umschalten` (switch over spindle) p. 5-14; operating mode splitting p. 3-11, 5-4; automatic work area partition wall p. 3-6, 3-11.
- Panel functions: `Antriebe ein/aus` (drives on/off) p. 5-8, 5-35; handwheel p. 5-8; `Zustimmtaste` (enabling button) p. 3-10, 5-8; `SPS-Tasten` (PLC keys) p. 5-10; `Zyklus Start` / `Zyklus stoppen` / `Vorschub anhalten` p. 5-12; spindle jog / rotate / stop p. 5-12 to 5-14; feed and speed override p. 5-27; foot switch p. 3-5, 5-20 (chuck actuation p. 5-20).
- Safety I/O: `Not-Aus` p. vi, 3-10, 5-10, 5-39; key switch (setting-up mode p. 5-38, bar machining p. 5-7); guard hood lock/unlock p. 5-9, manual unlock p. 5-20; automatic guard hood p. 3-7; work area position switches p. 5-38; speed monitoring p. 3-12; protection zones p. 6-25.
- Hydraulics/clamping: clamping pressure, stroke, pressure reducing valve p. 6-11; chuck type / clamping cylinder parameters p. 6-17; two-pressure clamping p. 3-7; pressure gauge p. 5-5.
- Auxiliaries: fresh-oil (total-loss) lubrication p. 8-3; lubrication unit, cooling unit, supply cabinet p. 3-5; spindle motor coolant p. 3-17, 8-4; coolant through spindle p. 3-7; chip conveyor forward/reverse p. 5-16 and its motor protection switch p. 5-5; operating hours counter p. 5-5; fault lamp p. 5-7.
- Options with I/O: pick-up device and ejector p. 5-18, 6-32 to 6-34; bar feeder p. 5-16, 6-44/6-45; bar loading magazine p. 6-46/6-47; tool measuring device p. 6-37; overload clutch p. 6-49; workpiece seating check p. 6-50; signal lamp p. 6-48.
