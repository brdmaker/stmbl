## For the retrofit

- Axes per side: X1/X2 cross slides 232 mm travel, slide inclined 45° (p. 2-3, 2-8); Z1 = turret slide on side 1, 380 mm (p. 2-3, 2-7); on side 2 the spindle motor/headstock itself travels longitudinally ("Längshub Spindelmotor (Z2)") 1165 mm (p. 2-3; HZ2 "Hub Spika" 1165 on p. 2-7), while table 2.5 also lists HZA "Hub Z2" 380 mm for turret 2 -- the naming of turret-2 Z vs. headstock-2 travel must be checked against the machine.
- Max. distance between spindles 1361 mm (LS, p. 2-3/2-7); A1 123,5 and A2 503,5 mm, F 520 mm (p. 2-7) -- relevant for the side 1 to side 2 part transfer (headstock 2 moves toward spindle 1).
- Feed motors: Indramat MAC 93 A (X, 2000 min⁻¹, 9,2 Nm S1 / 26 Nm) and MAC 93 B (Z, 3000 min⁻¹, 14,5 Nm S1 / 38,3 Nm), direct 1:1, ball screws 32x10 (X) and 40x10 (Z): 10 mm per motor revolution -> X 20 m/min and Z 30 m/min theoretical at max. motor speed; rapids set to 16 / 24 m/min; feed forces 4000 / 8000 N (p. 2-3, 2-8). (The mechanical manual lists MDD 093 A/B motors; types differ, check nameplates.)
- Spindles 1 and 2: integrated AC spindle motors, 18 kW S1, 202 Nm, 25-4000 min⁻¹, base speed 850 min⁻¹; spindle nose 170 h5 flat flange, bore 65 mm, bar capacity 51 mm (p. 2-3, 2-4). Spindle motors are liquid-cooled (contents 3.7.4).
- C axis (option): 100 min⁻¹, 202 Nm -- uses the spindle motor (p. 2-3).
- Turrets: 2 x 12 stations, VDI 40 (DIN 69880), 30° index, 0,2 s per station, direction-logic (shortest path) indexing (p. 2-3). Driven tools option: up to 12 per turret, 3000 min⁻¹, 5,3 kW / 24 Nm at 36% ED, 2,5 kW / 14,5 Nm S1, collet max. 16 mm (p. 2-3, 2-5).
- Chuck 200 mm, max. turning diameter 230 mm, swing 305 mm over cover / 310 mm, centre height 470 mm (p. 2-3, 2-6). Machine weight 9 500 kg, 5200x2700x1900 mm.
- Machine zero points M1/M2 on the spindle axes at the spindle noses (chuck mounting faces, as drawn), tool carrier reference points T1/T2 and tool reference points N1/N2 (p. 2-7) -- reference frame for LinuxCNC tool tables per side.
