## 8.1 Maintenance (page 8-3)

Like all machines, and like all of us, the robust CTX lathe also needs regular care and maintenance.

**Note** (pointing-hand pictogram): Please take the following instructions very seriously and very precisely, and be sure to follow the maintenance plan below and the sign `"Schmierstellen"` (lubrication points) on the machine.

## 8.2 Fill levels, checking (`Füllstände, Kontrolle`)

You should carry out the following work before every shift:

- **Fill level of hydraulic unit (`Füllstand Hydraulik-Aggregat`)**

  On the right side of the machine, at the bottom of the hydraulic cabinet, is the sight glass for the hydraulic oil level. The oil level must be above the red line.

  **Caution:** Never switch the machine on if it has too little hydraulic oil. In that case there is a leak somewhere in the closed hydraulic system. Please call your service.

- **Fill level of fresh-oil lubrication (`Füllstand Frischölschmierung`)**

  The tank for the fresh-oil lubrication is in the supply cabinet on the rear of the machine. It is easy to see through the grille. The oil must be above the black mark.

  If there is too little oil in the tank, please top up an appropriate quantity, see 3.7.2, "Lubricant table" on page 3-16.

- **Fill level of the coolant (`Füllstand des Kühlschmiermittels`)**

  The sight glass for the coolant level is on the coolant tank of the chip conveyor. The sight glass should be filled up to the line.

  **Caution:** If necessary, top up coolant in the correct mixing ratio (check beforehand with the refractometer if necessary).

- **Fill level of bar guide / bar feed (option) (`Füllstand Stangenführung / Stangenvorschub (Option)`)** (page 8-4)

  Check the oil level of the bar guide and the bar feed.

- **Fill level of cooling unit (`Füllstand Kühlaggregat`)**

  The tank for the coolant is in the supply cabinet on the right side of the machine, see 5-5. The fluid level must be above the mark.

  If there is too little coolant in the tank, please top up an appropriate quantity, see 3.7.2, "Lubricant table" on page 3-16.

## 8.3 Work area, checking and cleaning (`Arbeitsraum, Kontrolle und Reinigung`) (page 8-4)

- After every shift, clean the work area with the hand brush
- Grease the chuck/collet (with high-performance grease paste)

**Note** (pointing-hand pictogram): If you find damage during the following visual checks, please call your in-house service.

- Check chuck/collet for damage
- Check the guideway cover for damage
- Clean the control panel, screen and signal lamps
- Check foot switches for damage, clean if necessary
- Check the window of the guard door for damage, clean if necessary.

**Caution: Corrosion on the guide rails (`Korrosion an den Führungsschienen`).** During longer standstill periods, e.g. holidays, long weekends etc., the guide rails can corrode.

- Clean the guide rails before such standstill periods.
- Oil the guide rails by traversing the axes to the end positions and setting additional lubrication pulses with the `M 49` command.
- Also set additional lubrication pulses after longer standstill periods.

## 8.5 Lubricants and coolants (`Schmierstoffe und Kühlschmiermittel`) (pages 8-7 to 8-10)

### 8.5.1 Filling quantities (`Füllmengen`) (page 8-7)

- At least 100 l hydraulic oil (= one tank filling)
- 20 l slideway oil (for topping up the fresh-oil tank, capacity = 3 l). With automatic lubrication the consumption is approx. 0.5 l per 100 operating hours.
- 310 l coolant (= capacity of chip conveyor + coolant tank)
- At least 1 kg high-performance grease paste for greasing the chuck (must be greased every 8 operating hours).

**Oils for bar-feed devices (`Öle für Materialnachschubeinrichtungen`):**

| Make (`Fabrikat`) | Oil (`Öl`) | Quantity l (`Menge l`) |
|---|---|---|
| SAMECA | ISO CB 68 | 115 |
| IRCO | ISO CC 100 | 60 |
| FMB | ISO CC 150 | 120 |
| IEMCA | ISO CC 150 | 70 |

**Caution:** Coolant and slideway oil must not impair each other's function. The slideway oil must behave demulsifying towards the coolant. With different suppliers for coolant and slideway oil, experience shows it is advisable to have the suppliers guarantee in writing the compatibility of the lubricants with the coolant.

The pH value of the coolant used must be in the range 8.5 - 9.5.

**Caution: The mixing ratio according to the manufacturer's data must be observed.**
- e.g. too thin: machine guideways and workpieces oxidise
- e.g. too thick: insufficient cooling effect

Further information is available from your coolant manufacturer.

### 8.5.4 Coolant for the integrated spindle motors (page 8-9)

The cooling medium for the spindle motors is water with an addition of glycol. Mixing ratio:
- 95 % water
- 5 % glycol as corrosion protection

Recommendation, e.g.:
- Tyfocor, ethylene-glycol based, from Tyforop Chemie GmbH, Hamburg

**Warning: Tyfocor or an equivalent coolant is harmful to health! Take care when handling such media; observe the manufacturer's instructions.**

### 8.5.5 Handling lubricants and coolants (page 8-10)

**Warning: Oils, lubricants and coolants.** Because of their constituents (additives) these substances are a possible hazard to health and the environment.

Selection and use lie solely in the hands of the operator (company). An operating instruction to be drawn up by the operator, regulating the handling of these substances, should contain:

- designation of the substances
- hazards to people and the environment
- protective measures and rules of conduct, e.g.
  - wear protective gloves of resistant plastic
  - avoid contact with skin and eyes
  - do not inhale vapours and mist
  - fire, open flames and smoking are prohibited
- behaviour in case of danger, first aid
- proper disposal

Please clarify in good time how you can dispose of used lubricants and coolants in an environmentally sound way.

Lubricant vapours can arise during turning. For their removal Gildemeister offers oil mist separators.

## For the retrofit

- `M 49` = command for additional slideway-lubrication pulses (used with axes traversed to the end positions to oil the guide rails before/after long standstill) (p. 8-4). The new control needs an equivalent M-code / lube-pulse output.
- Lubrication: automatic fresh-oil (total-loss) lubrication with metering elements (`Dosierelemente`) feeding slides and ball screws; 3 l tank in the supply cabinet at the rear, ~0.5 l / 100 h (p. 8-5, 8-7). The control must pulse this pump periodically; level monitoring is not described here.
- Hydraulic unit: sight glass at the bottom of the hydraulic cabinet on the right side; never switch on with low oil (p. 8-3). Plan lists oil level, leakage, filter, temperature checks (p. 8-5).
- Spindle motor cooling unit (water/glycol) in the supply cabinet, right side (ref. page 5-5); fill level, filter, temperature (p. 8-4, 8-5, 8-9).
- Feed drives use toothed belt, pinion and coupling/encoder (`Kupplung/Geber`) (p. 8-5), i.e. motor-to-screw belt drive with a separate encoder per axis [uncertain whether encoder is motor-mounted].
- Tool turret: made by Sauter (separate Sauter documentation) (p. 8-5).
- Electrical cabinet has its own cooling unit with filter mat and condenser (p. 8-6).
- Options on this machine type mentioned: unloading device, chip conveyor, bar guide / bar feed (p. 8-4, 8-6).
- No control model, part-transfer sequence or PLC messages on these pages.
