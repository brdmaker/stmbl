## 2.1 Preliminary comments for maintenance

Prior to starting repair work observe "5. Maintenance and repairs" on page ix and "6 Guarantee" on page x.

The following components should only be repaired by the manufacturer:

- Spindle motors
- Slide roller guides
- Ball spindles and their bearings
- Turrets
- Chucking devices

## 2.2 Instructions for individual modules

### 2.2.1 Machine bed

Geometrically check the installation of the machine bed after 2500 operating hours and, if necessary, realign on the pressure screws.

The permissible tolerance is 0.01 mm longitudinally and cross based on 1 m.

**Prerequisite:**

Remove part of the sheet metal panelling to an extent which would allow unobstructed work.

**Necessary tools:**

- Spirit level with an accuracy of 1 division = 0.02 mm/m
- Alignment square / alignment bridge
- Open jaw spanner SW 17 / SW 24

**Concluding the work:**

Reinstall dismantled parts. Return the machine to its operational condition.

### 2.2.2 Spindle motor

**Aligning the spindle motor:**

Check the spindle motor position after every collision and after 2500 operating hours.

**Necessary help equipment**

- Alignment square / alignment bridge
- Spirit level 1 division = 0.02 mm/m
- Spindle measuring mandrel approx. 300 mm long
- Cylindrical mandrel centred on both ends, approx. 300 mm long
- Magnetic stand
- Dial gauge indicator, range 1 µm
- Various Allen keys
- Various open jaw spanners

Alignment is made in the MANUAL CONTROL / SETTING OPERATION operating mode.

- Refer to 2.2.1 "Machine bed" on page 2-3 for bed alignment
- To dismantle the clamping chuck refer to "Operating instructions chapter 6.5"
- Flange the alignment mandrel onto the spindle
- Fit the dial gauges for side positioning of the spindle motor on the slide
- Retract the alignment mandrels and read off any deviations. Permissible tolerance: refer to machine acceptance record.

If the deviations are beyond the tolerances, align the spindle motor as follows:

- Release the cover screws
- Lift the cover
- Remove the spindle motor screws
- Adjust the spindle motor with the setting screws until the measured values are within the tolerance range (tighten prior to measuring)
- Refit dismantled cover, remove the measuring means, fit chuck, return the machine to an operational condition.

### 2.2.3 Chucking device

The chucking cylinder and tensioning tube are glued together and form an assembly unit.

Caution (attention symbol): Under no circumstances fit workpiece stops or similar devices on the chucking cylinder or indexing housing, otherwise the chucking cylinder will block resulting in major damage to the machine.

**Dismantling:**

- Dismantle chuck and connection piece, refer to "operating instructions chapter 6.5"
- Switch off the machine at the OFF main switch
- Unscrew the supply and discharge lines, such as connection cables, hydraulic hoses, leak oil hose
- Remove the torque support
- Unscrew the fixing screws (if necessary, secure with a cable in a crane)
- Raise the clamping cylinder and tensioning tube and lay up on a suitable surface

**Assembly:**

- Clean the screw fitting flange
- Check the unit flange concentricity (radial 5 µm, axial 5 µm)
- Raise the chucking cylinder with tensioning tube and fit to the flange
- Slightly tighten the fixing screws and align the chucking cylinder (10 µm radial)
- Tighten the fixing screws with 90 nM (as printed; presumably 90 Nm)
- Fit the supply and discharge lines, such as hydraulic hoses, leak oil hose and connection cables. Support the connection piece tightening torque (pressure oil hose) with open jaw spanner, in order to avoid overtightening the thread on the chucking cylinder.
- Fit the torque support such that the rotary transmission leadthrough is fully relieved.
- Switch on the machine and select MANUAL CONTROL / SETTING OPERATION operating mode.
- Actuate the chucking cylinder several times (forward - return) to vent the system.
- Fit the chuck, refer to "Operating instructions chapter 6.5"
- Refit dismantled cover, return the machine to an operational condition

**Note:**

Refer to the manufacturers instructions for a precise description of the chucking cylinder!

### 2.2.4 Slides

#### 2.2.4.1 Setting the axial bearings

The bearing setting allows and axial play of 3 - 6 µm. The bearing is lubricated with fresh oil at a rate of 0.03 or 0.06 cm³ per lubrication pulse.

#### 2.2.4.2 Changing an axial bearing

Extreme cleanliness forms the prerequisite for the perfect running of ball thread spindles when fitting the new bearing.

- Move to reference point
- Unclamp all tools from the tool holders
- Move slides to the +X, +Z end position
- Release screws in the slide plates
- Move the slide into position approx. 300 mm from the headstock
- Switch off the machine
- Dismantle the fixing screws from the slide plate
- Push the slide plate to the end position
- Dismantle the screws from the pillow block bearing

Caution (attention symbol): When working on the X feed motor firstly secure the radial facing slide using suitable means to prevent sagging (e.g. support with a block of wood). Following this the X feed motor can be undone.

Page 2-7, figures (sectional drawings, no position numbers or legend):

- "Cross slide X1 or X2 axial bearing": section through the X ball screw end bearing, screw axis inclined (slant-bed cross slide), showing the bearing pack, locking nut and the screw end.
- "Longitudinal slide Z1 or Z2 axial bearing": section through the Z ball screw drive end with motor flange/coupling at left, ball nut on the slide, and an enlarged detail circle of the fixing of the bearing/nut housing with cap screws.

Caution (attention symbol, p. 2-8): Following a collision check the screws which fix the ball screw spindles to the slides for elongation. Exchange if elongation is established.

## 2.3 Putting out of commission

Putting out of commission is generally done in these steps:

- Disconnect the machine from the electrical supply, if necessary from other central supply networks
- Dispose of all oil and coolant
- Transport in accordance with the instructions contained in the transport instructions
- Dismantle the machine and use the material left over, this primarily being
  - Cast and steel elements from machine and housing
  - Electronic components from the control components
  - Copper cable, plastic from the electric components

For disposal observe the individually applicable national regulations. This applies in particular to (for types refer to 8.5-2 "lubricants and coolant" on page 8-8):

- Lubricating oil
- Hydraulic oil
- Cooling, lubricating agents
- Coolant

## For the retrofit

- Linear axes are named X1/Z1 and X2/Z2 (cross slides X1/X2, longitudinal slides Z1/Z2), one pair per side, each driven by a ball screw with an axial (end) bearing; there is an "X feed motor" per cross slide (p. 2-6, 2-7). Each side's LinuxCNC config therefore needs at least X and Z.
- Warning for motor work: the radial facing (X) slide sags when the X feed motor is removed, so it must be supported first (p. 2-6). For the retrofit this means the X axes need a holding brake or must be supported whenever the drive is disabled or swapped. The manual does not say whether a brake is fitted.
- Homing exists ("Move to reference point", p. 2-6); +X/+Z is the end position away from the work (p. 2-6), which tells you the axis sign convention.
- Each side has a spindle motor that is mounted on the slide and aligned with setting screws (p. 2-4), a hydraulic chucking cylinder with a rotary transmission leadthrough and leak-oil hose, and an "indexing housing" (p. 2-5). The indexing housing suggests spindle indexing/C-axis, but no details are given. The new control must operate chuck clamp/unclamp (forward/return) and needs a jog/manual mode for it (used to vent the system, p. 2-5).
- Turrets are fitted (listed as manufacturer-repair items, p. 2-3); there is no turret detail in these pages.
- Slide lubrication is pulsed: 0.03 or 0.06 cm³ fresh oil per lubrication pulse to the axial bearings (p. 2-6). The new control must drive the lube pump pulses (the pages give no interval).
- After a collision, check the screws fixing the ball screws to the slides for stretch, and the spindle motor alignment (p. 2-4, 2-8). This is relevant when tuning stmbl following-error/current limits.
- Hydraulics are documented in 8.8 (p. 8-14) and lubricants in 8.5-2 (p. 8-8), which are not in this scan set. Nothing on part transfer between the two sides appears in these pages.
