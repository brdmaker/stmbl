1. "Signal ?" -> branches:

## For the retrofit

- Chuck valves (4-59): each side has one double-solenoid 4/3 valve, 4WE6 E5X AG24 (24 V DC coils, 4-61). Left W24 (valve 14.1): Y 18241 = open left jaws, Y 18251 = close left jaws. Right W42 (valve 14.2): Y 24251 = open right jaws, Y 24241 = close right jaws. Spring-centred valve; the cylinders have built-in check valves, so the clamp is held with valves de-energised (per symbol, [uncertain]).
- Chuck monitoring inputs (4-59): per cylinder two stroke switches, left S 18121 (b1), S 18111 (b2); right S 24121 (b1), S 24111 (b2). Clamping-pressure switches F 18041 (left) and F 24041 (right) on the pressure reduction valves 24.1/24.2 (ZDRHD6 ... /200-63, adjustable "p = 5-60 bar"), monitoring channel A and B via shuttle plates 25.1/25.2. The control should require pressure OK + correct end switch before allowing spindle start / after clamp commands.
- Pressure switch adjustment (Rexroth insert, PDF p. 55-57): switching point via spindle pos. 12 (signal too early: out ~1/4 turn; too late: in ~1/4 turn); reduced (clamping) pressure via spindle pos. 4.
- Chuck hardware (4-55, 4-57, 4-58): left BERG OVR 110-32 closed-centre cylinder, right BERG OVRK 110-32 with rotary feedthrough; partially open centre draw tube. Supply lines P3/T3 (left) and P5/T5 (right) from the pressure supply unit.
- Part transfer between sides: the two chucks have independent valves and switches, so sync logic must interlock: close receiving chuck (Y 24241 or Y 18251), confirm its stroke switch + F x4041, then open releasing chuck (Y 18241 or Y 24251). Nothing on these pages describes a transfer sequence itself.
