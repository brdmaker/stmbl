## Page 4-3: Note

**Note:**

The machine is represented in the following chapters 4 to 6 in the form of drawings and part lists. This is intended to support repairs and maintenance work. The parts lists assist in determining defective parts with the help of the position number.

Prior to undertaking repair work observe the instructions contained in chapter 2 and, if necessary, involve the Gildemeister service.

## Assembly groups (page 4-5)

The machine bed comprises of the following assembly groups (refer to drawing on previous page):

1. **Machine bed**
2. **Integrated spindle motor, left and right**
3. **X1 axis feed motor**
4. **X2 axis feed motor**
5. **Z1 axis feed motor**
6. **Z2 axis feed motor and slide**
7. **Compound slide X1/Z1**
8. **Slide X2**
9. **Left and right hand chucking device**
   The chucking device comprises of:
   - hydraulically actuated chucking cylinder
   - Connection / tension tube
   - Chuck / collets
10. **Tool holder:**
    The tool holder serves to accept tools.
    In the machine version with driven tools the tool is driven via a servo motor mounted on the tool holder.
11. **Foot switch for left and right clamping**
12. **Supply cabinet containing the following components:**
    - Switch cabinet
    - Hydraulic unit
    - Lubrication unit
    - Cooling unit for integrated spindle motors
13. **Operating elements and monitor**
14. **Chip conveyor (optional)**

## For the retrofit

Only what pages 4-1 to 4-28 (as present in PDF pages 1-20) say:

- **Axes and naming** (4-5): four linear axes, X1/Z1 on the left compound slide (item 7 "Compound slide X1/Z1"), X2 on "Slide X2" (item 8), Z2 as "Z2 axis feed motor and slide" (item 6). The figure on 4-4 puts the Z2 motor at the far right end of the bed; the bed carries a "roller guide, longitudinal for spindle motor base" (4-7 pos. 10), which suggests the right headstock moves on Z2 and the X2 slide has no Z of its own [uncertain: inferred from the drawing and legend, not stated in text]. If so, part transfer between the sides is done by moving the right spindle (Z2) toward the left spindle, so the two "independent" controllers must share Z2 position/handshake during transfer.
- **Two spindles** (4-5 item 2): "Integrated spindle motor, left and right"; each has a cooling circuit fed from the cooling unit in the supply cabinet (4-5 item 12). Motor data is in 4.3 (pages 4-9 to 4-16), which is not in PDF pages 1-20.
- **X1 (cross) drive** (4-21 to 4-23): ball screw "cross 32X10, right" (32 mm dia, 10 mm pitch, right-hand) (pos. 50); motor INDRAMAT AC servomotor MDD 093 A-N-020-N2L-110PA1 (pos. 71), via KTR ROTEX GS 24/28 98 SHA coupling, bores 18 x 30 / 4 x 30 [uncertain bore reading] (pos. 58). Roller guides for cross and longitudinal motion.
- **Z1 (longitudinal) drive** (4-27): ball screw "longitudinal Z1 40X10" (40 mm dia, 10 mm pitch) (pos. 1); motor INDRAMAT MDD 093 B-N-030-N2L-110PA0 (pos. 15), KTR ROTEX GS 24/28 coupling 22 x 30 / 24 x 30 (pos. 8); fixed bearing INA 2575 LTN at motor end, INA PSHE 30 pillow block at the far end.
- **Motors/encoders**: both are Indramat MDD 093 AC servomotors (ordinarily resolver-feedback motors for Indramat DDS/DKS drives; the pages do not describe the feedback, so check the motor plate before choosing stmbl feedback). The "A-N-020" vs "B-N-030" codes differ (rated speed 2000 vs 3000 min^-1 by Indramat convention [uncertain, not in manual]). No separate linear scales appear in these lists.
- **Limit/reference switches**: Balluff BES516-325-S4-Y inductive proximity switches with plug, on "bracket for proximity detectors X interrogation" (4-21 pos. 5, 10) reading a switch plate (pos. 15), and for Z1 (4-28 pos. 25, 26) reading "switch plate for compound slide rest Z1" (pos. 29). Mechanical stops: X stop (4-21 pos. 2), Stopp Z (4-7 pos. 3), buffer stops (4-7 pos. 4, 4-21 pos. 1, 60, 4-27 pos. 9).
- **Tool holder** (4-5 item 10, 4-23/4-24 pos. 90, 95): SAUTER 12-position disc turret, either "Turret for 12 fixed tools, assembly group 05..481.520" or "LH turret for 12 driven tools, assembly group 05.450.020 ID-NO. 081812". With driven tools, the tool is driven by a servo motor mounted on the tool holder (4-5). Indexing/clamping details are not on these pages (see 4.10/4.11 hydraulic plan and equipment list, pages 4-47/4-49).
- **Chucking** (4-5 items 9, 11): hydraulically actuated chucking cylinders with draw tube and chuck/collets left and right; foot switches for left and right clamping are inputs the control must handle (valves and pressure switches are in 4.15, not in these pages).
- **Supply cabinet** (4-5 item 12): switch cabinet, hydraulic unit, lubrication unit, spindle-motor cooling unit; plus optional chip conveyor (item 14). Details in 4.8-4.15 (later pages).
- **Cable chain** for X1/Z1: Kabelschlepp 0650K, LS=845, BK=120, KR=115, on IGUS C-rail 2000 mm (4-21 pos. 20, 4-22 pos. 48): relevant if motor/encoder cables are replaced.
