## For the retrofit

- **Z2 axis (longitudinal, side 2)**: ball screw 40X10 (40 mm dia., 10 mm pitch per designation "40X10") (p. 4-31, pos. 1), driven via KTR ROTEX GS 24 coupling (bores 22 / 24 mm) by an Indramat AC servomotor MDD 093 B-N-030-N2L-110PA0 (pos. 15). The Z2 axis moves the "spindle motor base, moveable" (pos. 26), i.e. on side 2 the headstock travels in Z (relevant for part transfer between the sides) (p. 4-32).
- **X2 axis (radial facing slide 2)**: ball screw "cross 32X10, right" (32 mm, 10 mm pitch, right-hand) (p. 4-40, pos. 50); Indramat MDD 093 A-N-020-N2L-110PA1 (pos. 71, p. 4-41); KTR ROTEX GS 24 coupling (pos. 58); roller guides on a V-ledge (pos. 45, 48).
- Motor/coupling ratio is direct (coupling, no belt shown) for both axes: 10 mm per motor revolution [inferred from the parts lists; no gear listed]. MDD motor type codes (feedback type is encoded by Indramat, not stated in these pages); no separate linear scales appear in either parts list.
- **Reference / limit sensing**: Balluff proximity detectors BES516-325-S4-Y with plug connection on switch plates: "Z interrogation 2" (Z2, p. 4-32/4-33, pos. 39, 45) and "X interrogation" (X2, p. 4-39, pos. 10, 15, 20). Mechanical end stops: buffer stops 156--07-0011/000 and RH buffer bar (Z2), X stop (X2).
- **Turret (tool holder 2)**: Sauter disk turret, RH, 12 stations; variants for 12 fixed tools (assembly group 05.481.520) or 12 driven tools (05.450.020, ID 081972) (p. 4-41). Turret indexing drive/sensors are not described on these pages.
- **Turret lock/release hydraulics** (p. 4-47): per tool holder one double-solenoid directional valve 17.0 with solenoids WS 17-1 (lock) and WS 17-2 (release), behind a 50 bar pressure reducing valve 13.0; both holders use the same solenoid names (one valve stack each). The control must switch lock/release around each index.
- **Hydraulic pressure supply** (p. 4-43/4-45): Rexroth 100 l unit, adjustable vane pump 1PV2V7, 2.2 kW 1450 rpm motor 230/400 V 50 Hz (motor label "M 0901" [uncertain]), 70 bar, 19 l/min theoretical; accumulator 2 l, precharge 55 bar; pressure limiting valve 61 DBDS 6 G1X/80B. Control inputs: pressure switch 6.1 Rexroth HEG 8 (range 5-60 bar, label "F 09051" [uncertain]), line filter clogging switch "F 09061" [uncertain], return filter switch "F xxxx", optional level switch 1.2. Outlets P1/T1, P2/T2, P3/T3 (likely one per consumer group, not stated).
- **Cable chains**: Z2 chain 0650K, LK = 2015 (2205), BK 210, KR 300 (p. 4-32); X2 chain 0650K, LS = 845, BK 120, KR 115 (p. 4-40) — length limits for new motor/encoder cables.
- Lubrication and part transfer are not covered on these pages (lubrication is in 4.12-4.14).
